Leaderboard plan: contrarian, not reckless

Every Hotspawn player who picks the same side scores the same points, so you only climb when you are right where the field is wrong. The plan below fades the crowd only when the underdog's estimated chance is high — judged by outside prices and our own line together, never by the crowd itself.

How to use it: take every VALUE play (likely winner that most players are against). Add CONTRARIAN plays when you need to gain ground. Use SITUATIONAL plays when you are chasing; they rest on verified roster changes, stand-ins or late replacements. Treat LONG SHOT underdogs as an occasional punt only when far behind with few matches left. S-tier: not published points · B-tier: 3 points · C-tier: 2 points (this payload). Higher-point calls carry more leaderboard leverage.

EV cost = expected points given up versus taking the published pick, measured on the published probabilities (points × gap in win chance). Underdog chance = heuristic average of the market and our eligible own line (separate estimates, not statistically independent evidence) (our line is dropped when Valve's roster is out of date). Crowd = Hotspawn's pick share, used here only to measure how contrarian a pick is.

The heuristic rule (v3: evidence eligibility)

  • VALUE crowd majority sits on the published favourite’s opponent → play the favourite.
  • CONTRARIAN underdog consensus ≥ 38%, every estimate ≥ 30%, and crowd share at least 10 points below its chance → play the underdog.
  • SITUATIONAL underdog consensus ≥ 30%, every estimate ≥ 25%, crowd share ≤ half its chance, a strong/moderate verified factor against the favourite and none against the underdog → play the underdog.
  • LONG SHOT underdog ≥ 20% with ≤ 10% of players → keep the favourite (optional punt).
  • CHALK everything else → keep the favourite.
