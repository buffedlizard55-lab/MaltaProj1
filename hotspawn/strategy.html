Leaderboard plan: contrarian, not reckless

Every Hotspawn player who picks the same side scores the same points, so you only climb when you are right where the field is wrong. The plan below fades the crowd only when the underdog's real chance is high — judged by outside prices and our own line together, never by the crowd itself.

How to use it: take every VALUE play (likely winner that most players are against). Add CONTRARIAN plays when you need to gain ground. Use SITUATIONAL plays when you are chasing; they rest on verified roster changes, stand-ins or late replacements. Treat LONG SHOT underdogs as an occasional punt only when far behind with few matches left. S-tier matches are worth 7 points versus 2 for C-tier, so the S-tier calls matter most.

EV cost = expected points given up versus simply taking the favourite (points × gap in win chance). Underdog chance = consensus of the market and our own line (our line is dropped when Valve's roster is out of date). Crowd = Hotspawn's pick share, used here only to measure how contrarian a pick is.

The rule (v2)

  • VALUE crowd majority sits on the consensus underdog → play the favourite.
  • CONTRARIAN underdog consensus ≥ 38%, every estimate ≥ 30%, and crowd share at least 10 points below its chance → play the underdog.
  • SITUATIONAL underdog consensus ≥ 30%, every estimate ≥ 25%, crowd share ≤ half its chance, a strong/moderate verified factor against the favourite and none against the underdog → play the underdog.
  • LONG SHOT underdog ≥ 20% with ≤ 10% of players → keep the favourite (optional punt).
  • CHALK everything else → keep the favourite.

Honesty note: rule v1 used the market alone and let weak factors (for example "busy schedule") count. Graded on TWC day 1 it would have gone 1/4, so it was replaced by v2 before publication. v2 goes 4/4 on the same four matches, but that is in-sample with n = 4 and proves nothing; the live grading below is the real test.

What day 1 taught us

  • The only upset (9z over Aurora) came from the side with the largest gap between our own line and the crowd: the official-points line gave 9z about 39% while the market said 27% and only 5% of Hotspawn players picked 9z. A correct pick there gained 7 points on 95% of the field.
  • The three favourites the crowd agreed with all won 2-0, so 'chalk' cost nothing on day 1; contrarian picks pay off only when the underdog's real chance is large, which is why the rule requires a minimum probability before fading the crowd.
  • Roster churn is a usable signal but not proof: Aurora's July rebuild did not stop a 3rd place at EPL S24, yet the team still lost 0-2 here. Treat onboarding as a tiebreaker on close matches, not as a reason to back long shots.

The upset: 9z 2-0 Aurora

Aurora rebuilt in July 2026 — Jimpphat joined on 3 Jul and kyxsan became in-game leader on 8 Jul (coach ash). That is about three months before TWC, and the new five had just finished 3rd at ESL Pro League S24, beating 9z 2-0 there on 3 Oct. In Malta 9z won Inferno 13-11 and Nuke 13-1 (luchov 1.61).
