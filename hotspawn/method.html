Method, sources & read times

1. Fixture list (Hotspawn)

Hotspawn Play is an Angular app that ships its data in the page as <script id="ng-state">. The collector reads all three CS2 surfaces and takes, per fixture: id, tournament, tier, stage, points breakdown, teams, scheduled_start_time and lock_time (epoch ms), plus the crowd pick share (kept as "ownership").

2. Probability ladder

  1. Pinnacle whole-match moneyline from the public guest JSON behind pinnacle.com CS2; American prices → decimal; margin removed proportionally (p = (1/o) / Σ(1/o)).
  2. Secondary esportsbook relay from bo3.gg, only after the HLTV gap attempt. A named, active, same-book two-sided line, labelled relayed and not verified directly with the sportsbook.
  3. Polymarket match-winner markets (tag "counter strike 2"), outcome prices normalised to 100% (not asserted to be a fresh midpoint or executable bid/ask). A prediction market, labelled as such.
  4. Our own line: P(A) = 1 / (1 + 10^(−k·ΔPoints/400)) with Valve's official points (standings 2026-10-05). k = 0.4598 (fitted on Valve's published match results); trained on 13609 Valve-rated matches, held out on 901 (window 2026-09-07..2026-10-05): log-loss 0.6395 vs 0.6541 for Valve's raw curve; Brier 0.2234 vs 0.2237.
  5. Research fallback (rung E): the same own-line formula, applied only when the rungs above cannot resolve an open fixture and a team name maps to more than one roster row in Valve's standings. The row is chosen by documented roster overlap with the lineup HLTV lists for that exact match (≥ 3 shared nicknames and strictly more than any other same-name row; every candidate row is linked on the row and in fallback_83090640.json). No book, prediction market or aggregator price is invented for these fixtures: HLTV's betting table, Pinnacle, Polymarket and the esportsbook relays are checked first and their absence is recorded. Roster changes versus the snapshot are flagged, never smoothed away.
  6. Unresolved: blank pick, flagged, listed on the Unresolved page with the research trail.

Joins require both teams to match after verified aliases (aliases.json) the competition family/region/edition and a kickoff within 6 h, with no equally close or nearer same-pair rematch; any gap is flagged. Unknown scope, nonfinite/terminal prices, closed markets and quotes older than 8 h are rejected, not quietly used. HLTV ranks are shown as a cross-check (HLTV ranking, status observed).

Some bo3.gg quote records give only a numeric bookmaker ID. We preserve that identifier but leave the operator name blank, flag BOOKMAKER_UNVERIFIED, and do not infer identity from affiliate links. These are secondary source-implied estimates, not direct-book verification; automatic contrarian fades are disabled for them.

3. Leaderboard plan

See Leaderboard plan; thresholds live in model.py. Situational factors come from research/notes.json (HLTV links, read times, strength) plus an automatic "new five" check on Valve's per-team match tables.

4. Automation

GitHub Actions workflow is scheduled every 4 hours (best effort; captures older than 8 hours are marked stale): collect → calibrate → model → build → per-row audit → tests → required real-browser phone/tablet check; pages are committed only when the tests pass, otherwise the last good pages stay up and the failure is visible in the run log.
