Limitations that apply to every pick

  • Four-hour scheduled snapshots, not live odds. Scheduling and source access are best effort; a capture older than eight hours is marked stale. Check each read time and absolute UTC lock.
  • Both teams, competition, series format (when independently known), freshness and rematch identity must agree. Rejected or stale quotes do not silently drive picks.
  • HLTV is tried for non-featured fixtures first. A refusal is recorded; a 403/429 circuit breaker is not a successful read. Then esportsbook relays and other external lines are checked automatically.
  • The own line is fitted to Valve’s whole-match results, not map probabilities. No extra BO3 transform is applied. Missing teams, unknown formats, known roster mismatches and ratings older than 45 days withhold that fallback.
  • Research-fallback (rung E) picks rest on a dated Valve snapshot with a documented roster-overlap identity rule and no market cross-check at all. They are the softest published lines: every negative price check and every roster delta is printed on the row above.
  • Direction-only picks (research rung F) publish a side from HLTV's own one-sided verdict and nothing else. They carry no win percentage, no expected-points cost and no leaderboard leverage, because a number for an unranked, unrated opponent would be invented rather than sourced.
  • Roster news can already be priced into the market. A three-month-old rebuild is context only; no unverified travel, sleep, injury or fatigue adjustment is made.
  • Hotspawn crowd percentages are ownership only. Small samples, rounding and correlated picks limit leaderboard analysis; low ownership never increases a team’s win probability.

Independent research, not affiliated with Hotspawn; not betting advice.
