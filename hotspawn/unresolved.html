Limitations that apply to every pick

  • Snapshots, not live odds: prices are read every 3 hours; a line can move or be pulled before lock. Every price shows its read time.
  • Pinnacle usually posts low-tier lines about a day ahead and can pull them close to start, so C-tier matches often sit on Polymarket or our own line and switch on a later refresh.
  • Our own line uses Valve's official points (published monthly; current file named in the snapshot bar) on a curve fitted to Valve's own results; it knows nothing about stand-ins or brand-new rosters — those are shown as factors.
  • HLTV blocks most automated reads, so HLTV facts (formats, rosters, stand-ins) come from this session's researched notes with links; new fixtures get HLTV links but no new notes until researched.
  • Day-3 Thunderpick deciders carry a hand-verified evidence file (hotspawn/research/day3_twc2026.json) holding raw inputs only — bookmaker decimal odds, Polymarket prices, Valve points and Hotspawn ownership — with a source URL and read time per row; every percentage on the page is recomputed at build time from those inputs.
  • If a fixture Hotspawn lists is missing from a snapshot, the board cannot pick it: it is named in the coverage-gap block on the unresolved page and in the executive summary, with the researched fallback, instead of being dropped.
  • Hotspawn's crowd share is shown as ownership only and changes until lock.

Independent research, not affiliated with Hotspawn; not betting advice. Probabilities come from outside prices (Pinnacle, Polymarket) or our own line from Valve's official standings — never from Hotspawn's crowd percentages, which appear only as "ownership" for the leaderboard plan.
