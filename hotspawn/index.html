Limitations that apply to every pick

  • HLTV blocks most automated reads, so HLTV facts (formats, rosters, stand-ins) come from this session's manual research with links; new fixtures get HLTV links but no new notes until researched.
  • Hotspawn's crowd share is shown as ownership only and changes until lock.
