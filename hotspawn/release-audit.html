Flagged for Review: limitations that a coverage count can hide

  • Hindsight: day-1 leaderboard “4/4” is a retrospective application of a later rule, not proof of four bets or picks made before lock. Do not use it as a backtest of the deployed strategy.
  • Small sample: the saved day-2 review has three frozen picks, two correct for both strategies; Aurora–NiP has no frozen pick and is excluded. Four results are not four archived predictions.
  • Prediction-market quality: a 50/50 quote is not affirmative support for either side. Trade volume does not establish current depth. The Aurora event page contains both series and map contracts and displayed 70¢ / 31¢ in this re-read; do not mix contract scopes or call that 101% pair a calibrated probability. The collector filters match-winner titles; executable spread/depth remain unverified.
  • Leaderboard objective: ownership gaps measure differentiation, not betting value or the probability of finishing first. The optimal risk depends on your deficit, remaining matches, opponents and streak rules. No strategy here guarantees a climb.
  • Refresh reliability: the existing three-hour workflow checks all three Hotspawn surfaces. Schedules can be delayed; check actual capture age. A prior phone script can return success when the browser is unavailable; release assurance requires an actual passed browser report.
  • Legacy repo checks: the initial 405-test run had two valuepicks coverage failures and one livelines deterministic-build error, outside the canonical board. These are not evidence that every archive is current. Separate PR #150 is not silently integrated by this session.
