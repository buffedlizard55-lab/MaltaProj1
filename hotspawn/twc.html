Contrarian, not reckless

  • Day 1: 9z’s 2-0 win over Aurora was real, but an upset does not by itself prove the favourite’s pre-match line was wrong. Keep result verification separate from prediction quality.
  • Day 2: Aurora won 2-0; Falcons, BetBoom and FURIA won 2-1. Falcons and FURIA advance; NIP and PARIVISION are out. There was no BetBoom 2-0 sweep.
  • For expected points, use the team with the larger current, source-backed probability. For a chaser, a credible underdog with low ownership can differentiate; the downside is the displayed expected-points cost, not a guaranteed leaderboard gain.
  • A conservative upset watch requires an eligible outside line giving the underdog at least 30%, ownership at least 10 percentage points lower, and at least 10 recorded crowd picks. These are transparent heuristic gates, not a fitted or proven ranking strategy.
  • Do not force several correlated upsets just to be different. One deliberate high-point fade may be enough; without the user’s rank, remaining points and the full field’s picks, the site cannot optimize the probability of winning the contest.
