How to climb the board on day 3

  • Day 1: all four favourites won except the one the market liked at about 73-76% - 9z 2-0 Aurora. The crowd had 5% on 9z; the outside market had 27-35%.
  • Day 2: the two favourites that had already won did it again (Falcons, FURIA), and both losers of day 1 who stayed alive did so by 2-0 over the teams that had lost too (Aurora, BetBoom). The gap between the top and bottom of each group was bigger than the gap the day-1 upset suggested.
  • Reading for day 3: the deciders are the only two matches left, both 1-1 vs 1-1, and both carry 7 points. Take the favourite (market side) for the headline pick, and keep the faded underdog as the leaderboard play where an outside price puts it above its crowd share - that is exactly the 9z case (31.5-34.8% priced vs 11% of players).
  • Contrarian but not stupid means: never fade a favourite just because the crowd is on it; fade only when at least two outside estimates, or one outside estimate plus our own line, put the underdog within reach and the crowd is far below that number.
