## 1. Method

1. Fixture list (88 rows, teams, card dates, lock times, tiers): captured by the repository's scheduled Hotspawn parser (`src/pickem_inventory.py` → `data/pickem_inventory.json` → `executive/data/fixtures.csv`); spot-checked against a live re-read of <https://play.hotspawn.com/cs2-pickems> on 2026-10-14 (~08:30Z): 25 open cards + 3 locked/started today (1079621, 1081111, 1080176) + 60 stale cards, header count 88. **Hotspawn's crowd "picked %" was not used anywhere as a probability input.**
2. Open fixtures (28): pick + win probability per row from `executive/data/analysis.csv`, built by the executive pipeline from outside sources only — primary bookmaker (Pinnacle), secondary bookmaker relay (EGamersWorld/bc.game), prediction market (Polymarket, labelled), and where no market prices a match, our own line originated from the official Valve Regional Standings file (pinned commit in the row's source links) and/or the official HLTV world ranking. Every row carries `source_links` to the exact inputs.
3. Stale fixtures (60): each card (teams + card date) matched to the exact official match record on the HLTV results page of the corresponding event (or Liquipedia for DACH CS Masters S5, which is not on HLTV). Winner, score, format and result date taken from that record; the match page URL is the row's source. The full research trail (event mapping, per-tournament pairings, the DraculaN evidence) is in [`data/c13ca42a/research_stale_results.md`](../data/c13ca42a/research_stale_results.md).
4. Invariants (enforced by `scripts/c13ca42a_build_fullpicks.py`, fail-closed, run by CI every 4 h and by `tests/test_c13ca42a.py`): 88 = 28 + 54 + 6 exactly; every verified row has a winner that is one of the two card teams, an `a-b` score, a result date, and an https official source; every unresolved row has **no** winner/score and a flag; every board row has at least one source link; all links https; both HTML pages contain all fixture ids.
