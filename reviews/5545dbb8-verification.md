## 6. Method notes a reviewer should confirm

1. **Never Hotspawn as a probability.** Hotspawn supplies fixture identity, tournament, tier, offered
   points and the UTC epochs only. Its crowd pick percentages are dropped at ingest
   (`collect.py → DROPPED_FIELDS`), are not stored anywhere in `executive/data/`, and an invariant fails
   the build if a crowd value appears in the JSON.
2. **De-vig.** Two-way American prices → decimal → `p = (1/o) / Σ(1/o)`, with the overround printed on
   every row that uses an outside price. Equal-pair placeholders (`1.80 / 1.80`) are rejected and the
   row falls to our own line with `FL-PLACEHOLDER-REJECTED`.
3. **Our own line.** `p = 1/(1+10^((Rb−Ra)/400))` over the two official standing values — the relation
   Valve publish in
   [`model/glicko.js`](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/blob/main/model/glicko.js)
   ("a rank delta of 400 points equals a 90% chance of victory"). Rows it drives are labelled
   *uncalibrated* in words, because it is.
4. **Identity rules.** A market quote is used only when **both** team names match as a pair (either
   order, through Hotspawn's spelling or an evidence-linked rename) **and** the quote's kickoff is
   within 15 minutes of the fixture's. An HLTV link is published only when the team pair **and** the
   significant words of the tournament name both agree with HLTV's own slug. Ambiguity produces a flag
   and a blank, never a guess.
5. **Provisional rows are bounds.** When a team is absent from the whole official file, the lowest
   published value in that same file is used as its *ceiling*, so the published number is a lower bound
   on the favourite's true probability. The row says so in words, not only in a code.
6. **Older captures are never relabelled.** Each row prints the capture minute of the read behind it;
   the freshness rules (`FL-STALE-CAPTURE` at >6 h, `FL-MARKET-STALE` at >12 h) decide whether that
   capture may drive a headline probability.
