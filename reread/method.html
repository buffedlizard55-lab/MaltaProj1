Why this page exists. The repository's boards publish prices and ratings that were copied into data files by earlier sessions. An audit flagged that most of those values were never re-read from the source afterwards. This tool re-reads them, on a schedule, and records what the source says now.

Policy

  • Hotspawn is read for fixture identity and series format only. Its crowd percentages are never parsed and never used as a probability.
  • A refusal (HTTP 403/429), a dead link (404) or an unparsable page is recorded as such. It is never filled in with an assumed value.
  • This tool never edits another board's data. A disagreement is published on Flagged for Review with the source link.
  • The 1.80/1.80 EGamersWorld pair is recognised as a placeholder, not a market.
  • A row whose source is re-read less often than every 6 hours is marked "reused"; the row keeps the timestamp of the run that actually read it.
  • Team names are compared against the evidence-backed rename map published by the line-analysis board (cs2/aliases.json); a name that only matches through such an alias is reported as alias:, never as a silent pass and never invented here.
