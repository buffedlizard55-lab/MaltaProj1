How to read a row

  • Market (de-vigged) — the two-way bookmaker price pair read from EGamersWorld's bc.game block, with the overround removed proportionally. Blank where no price was published.
  • Own model (VRS) — our own line, computed from Valve's official Regional Standings points with Valve's own Glicko expected-win function, then adjusted for the series format. Full method.
  • Pick — the external market favourite where a line exists; otherwise the favourite by our own model. Where neither exists, no pick is published.
  • Sources — Hotspawn fixture, the exact EGamersWorld page the price was read from, and the Valve standings file.
