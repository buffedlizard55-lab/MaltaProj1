Methodology

How every pick on this site is produced. Generated 2026-10-14T03:08:10Z.

What Hotspawn is used for

  • Fixture identity: team names, competition, tier/points, and the open CS2 Pick'em match set.
  • UTC start and lock epochs (payload scheduled_start_time/lock_time).
  • Never a probability input: Hotspawn crowd pick percentages are excluded everywhere.

Where probabilities come from

  • Market lines. Two-way de-vig of decimal odds: p(A) = (1/o_A) / (1/o_A + 1/o_B). Primary tier = direct bookmaker guest quote (Pinnacle, captured by the hourly slate pipeline). Aggregator tier = bookmaker odds as listed on EGamersWorld (bc.game), capture-stamped 2026-10-14 and flagged because we cannot re-verify them against the book ourselves. Placeholder prices (1.80/1.80) are rejected.
  • Our originated line. Every team gets rating points from official sources: HLTV world-ranking points and Valve Regional Standings (VRS) points. Each side becomes a per-map probability with the standard Elo logistic p = 1 / (1 + 10^((r_A − r_B)/400)), then a series probability BO3: p²(3−2p) · BO5: p³(10−15p+6p²) · BO1: p. When both sides exist the map/series probability is the equal-weight mean of the HLTV-based and VRS-based values (identical arithmetic to slate/collect.py). The 400 scale and equal weighting are project assumptions — not calibrated forecasts, and not Valve/HLTV formulas.
  • Series formats come from HLTV match pages (BO1/BO3/BO5 labels), linked per row.

Pick hierarchy

  1. Primary bookmaker line → market favourite.
  2. Aggregator-reported line (flagged) → market favourite.
  3. Originated rating line with a sourced BO format → series-probability favourite (labeled provisional — the model is not calibrated).
  4. Format unverified but ratings exist → map-level favourite as a provisional guide.
  5. One team has no verified rating anywhere → pick the rateable team, probability left blank.
  6. Neither team rateable and no market → no pick, flagged. (Currently 0 such matches.)

Rating inputs and name resolution

  • Valve VRS rows are taken from Valve's official standings_global_2026_10_05.md. Where that file lists duplicate names, the disambiguated roster-verified row is used (see IRREG-02).
  • HLTV world-ranking points come from hltv.org/ranking/teams.
  • Teams absent from both (e.g. LFO 10) are researched separately; each researched value records its own source URL and is flagged. Mixed snapshot dates across teams are flagged per row.
  • Evidence-linked name aliases (WROTBERRY→WBT, RoundsGG→ROUNDS, Envy→3DMAX, EAC→EA Copenhagen) carry their evidence URLs; alias-derived inputs are flagged on the row.

Limitations

  • Model probabilities are provisional and uncalibrated; do not treat them as official forecasts.
  • Aggregator-reported odds may drift or differ from the book’s own feed.
  • Market/model divergences are published as flags; the market favourite drives the pick when a line exists.
  • Analysis only — not betting advice.
