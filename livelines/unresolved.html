Limitations that apply to these rows

No sportsbook prices the low-tier CCT / Elisa fixtures
EGamersWorld prints exactly 1.80 / 1.80 for five different CCT Europe Series #12 Play-In fixtures on one page (banda chuya v 6666, WRAITH PCIFIC v Ryvex, Saint Sinners v MAYBE, Lilmix v Revise, mellren v Privateer Gaming) while printing real, different prices for other fixtures on the same pages. Identical pairs on unrelated matches are a listing placeholder, not a market — one of those fixtures, Ryvex v PCIFIC, has an independent Polymarket price that contradicts a coin-flip. The pairs are therefore rejected and flagged, never used as a number.
https://egamersworld.com/counterstrike/event/cct-europe-2026-series-12-play-in-1sGIL2gAYu
One fixture's opponent cannot be identified outside Hotspawn
For FORZE Reload v LFO 10 the only outside listing found prints the opponent cell empty (no name, no flag, no team link; the match URL slug is literally 'forze-reload-vs-'). LFO 10 appears nowhere in the official Valve Regional Standings file (380 teams) nor in HLTV's top 245, so no rating exists for it and only a bound can be published.
https://egamersworld.com/counterstrike/event/cct-europe-2026-series-11-_rW8GEyWK
Official rating snapshots are not intraday
Valve publishes the Regional Standings weekly; the newest official file at build time is the 2026-10-05 snapshot (380 entries, blob 2aa28053). HLTV's world ranking is weekly too (dated 2026-10-12 here) and lists only the top 245 teams. A match played today is therefore always rated from a snapshot several days old — flagged on every affected row, never hidden.
https://www.hltv.org/ranking/teams/2026/october/12
Series length is not always published
8 of 8 open fixtures carry a stated series length at build time (from the outside feed or the EGamersWorld schedule, which prints 'Bo3'). The rest stay blank and flagged rather than assumed.
https://egamersworld.com/counterstrike/event/cct-europe-2026-series-11-_rW8GEyWK
The board's own refresh is not guaranteed by the platform
GitHub Actions disables scheduled workflows on inactive repositories and runs them best-effort; this repository has many scheduled workflows competing for the same window, and one of them failed on main on 2026-10-14 (run 38511378749, 'Executive board refresh'). Every page here prints the age of its inputs instead of asserting freshness.
https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/MaltaProj1/actions/runs/38511378749
Hotspawn itself lists fixtures that can no longer be picked
81 of the 89 fixtures in the site payload are locked or past — most carry June dates the board never cleared, and one (1079546, Zomblers v Overtake Sector) disappeared from the board between the 02:06:30Z capture and the 04:31Z live read. No pick exists or can exist for those, so they are counted, not silently dropped.
https://play.hotspawn.com/cs2-pickems

What we did to try to resolve them

1. HLTV: the world ranking (read live 2026-10-14T04:33:30Z) and the repository's captured ranking page supply ranks and points for the top 245 teams; HLTV does not publish per-match prices, and its match pages are reachable only through the platform fetch tool, not the sandbox shell.
2. EgamersWorld (bc.game listing) event pages were read for both CCT events at 2026-10-14T04:36:30Z and 04:37:30Z: they resolve the series length (Bo3) for the CCT fixtures and identify the fixture whose opponent is missing, but they print the 1.80/1.80 placeholder for exactly the fixtures no book has priced.
3. Polymarket was read directly (Gothic v Lavked moneyline 50¢/51¢ at 04:38:05Z, volume $304); it prices the fixtures its traders care about and does not list the C-tier play-in matches at all.
4. Official Valve Regional Standings (GitHub, ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings) were pulled through the GitHub contents API: 380 entries, dated 2026-10-05, which is what the originated line is built on.

Independent analysis, not betting advice. Hotspawn supplies fixture identity only; its crowd percentages are never stored or used. Every number above names the page it was read from.
