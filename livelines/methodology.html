The basis ladder

T1 — primary bookmaker. A Pinnacle guest-market quote captured by this repo's pipeline. De-vigged: p = (1/odds) / Σ(1/odds). No T1 quote existed for any open fixture at build time, so every market row below is T2.

T2 — outside listing or prediction market. The newest readable price for the fixture from Polymarket (share price in USD 0-1, used directly as the market's own probability), Thunderpick prices relayed by bo3.gg, or the EGamersWorld listing of bc.game prices (de-vigged as above). Pairs that are identical across unrelated fixtures on the same page (1.80/1.80) are rejected as placeholders and published on the Flagged page instead.

T3 — our own originated line. p = 1/(1+10^((vrs_b − vrs_a)/400)) × 100, where vrs_a / vrs_b are the official Valve Regional Standings points for the two teams. The scale 400 points per 90% favourite is fitted at build time by grid search against the fixtures an outside market actually prices (0 fit rows, mean absolute error None percentage points), not assumed. If fewer than four such fixtures exist the project default of 400 points applies instead and the fit is marked unfitted.

T4 — bound only. If a team is absent from the official standings, the lowest published value in the same file is used as its ceiling. The known side's true win probability is then at least the published number, so no pick is published for that fixture — it is listed on Unresolved.

Rules the board enforces

· Hotspawn's crowd pick percentage is never read, stored or used — a build test fails if it appears.
· A pick is only published when the row has a probability for both sides from the basis in the basis column.
· No averaging of two sources into one number: the newest readable price for the fixture is published, older ones are shown as drift when they differ by ≥ 0.5 pp.
· Unknowns stay blank and are flagged; nothing is inferred from a name, a tier or a crowd vote.
· Every row carries the URL it came from; every source URL is listed with its read time on Sources.

What could be wrong

· Our line is a rating transform, not a calibrated model of CS2 outcomes, and the official ratings it uses are weekly.
· Prediction-market prices are last-traded prices on thin books (Gothic v Lavked traded $304 of volume at read time).
· Series length can change how a rating gap converts into a series probability; where no source states the length the row says so.
· A match can be postponed or cancelled after publication; the board prints lock times, and prices are read times, not guarantees.

Reproduce

python3 livelines/build.py rebuilds this board offline from the committed captures listed in data/provenance.json. python3 -m unittest tests.test_457629c4 runs the invariants. The live reads taken on 2026-10-14 are stored verbatim in data/live_reads.json with their URLs and read times.

Independent analysis, not betting advice. Hotspawn supplies fixture identity only; its crowd percentages are never stored or used. Every number above names the page it was read from.
