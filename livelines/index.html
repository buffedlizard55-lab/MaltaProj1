What this board is

One row for every match on the Hotspawn CS2 Pick'em board that is still open, each with the pick, both sides' win probability, the basis it came from and the URL behind every number. The slate (fixture identity and exact UTC lock epochs) comes from Hotspawn's own page payload; the probabilities come from outside sources only — Pinnacle guest markets, Polymarket, Thunderpick prices relayed by bo3.gg and the EGamersWorld/bc.game listing — and, where no sportsbook prices a match, from our own line built on the official Valve Regional Standings. Hotspawn's crowd percentage is never an input.
