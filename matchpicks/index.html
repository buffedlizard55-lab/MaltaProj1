Executive summary

What this is. Every Counter-Strike 2 fixture Hotspawn's Pick'em board lists, with the team to pick and the probability of each team winning. Probabilities come from outside sources only: a de-vigged bookmaker line where one exists (Pinnacle first, then the EGamersWorld aggregate), a labelled prediction market next, and otherwise our own line built from Valve's official Regional Standings or the official HLTV ranking Hotspawn is used for fixture identity, times and the published score — never for a probability.

Where the picks are: Open fixtures (pick now) · 80 captured fixtures incl. played & stale listings · Unresolved picks & limitations (24) · Flagged for review (112 item(s)) · Method & sources

Limitations, stated up front. Two of the sources this ladder prefers refused the automated read during this capture — HLTV's upcoming-match list and the EGamersWorld aggregate both answer the runner with HTTP 403 (recorded in every capture), so the length of a series comes from a hand-checked, linked official HLTV page (18 row(s) joined live this time, the rest from the research trail), and rows that would have used an aggregate price are labelled as our own line instead. The own line is built on Valve's newest published standings file, so between publications a team's live rating may have moved. Hotspawn publishes no best-of length and no usable score at all — its best_of and series_format fields are null for every fixture and every fixture is stuck at 0-0 “scheduled”, even ones from June. Everything that could not be confirmed stays blank and is flagged: Unresolved picks & limitations (24) lists every row without a pick, with the reason, and Flagged for review (112 item(s)) lists every irregularity with the rows it touches.

Freshness. Captured 2026-10-15T08:45:34Z; Hotspawn reports “72 matches this week” and a reset in 3d 16h 04m 15s. The board rebuilds automatically every 4 hours; the timestamp above is the time of the data on this page, not of your visit. Use the absolute UTC lock time on each row, not a relative countdown.
