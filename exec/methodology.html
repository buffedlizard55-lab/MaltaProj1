Method

Short version: a bookmaker price decides the pick when one exists; otherwise our own line from official ratings does; otherwise nothing is published. Every row prints both numbers when both exist, so a disagreement is visible rather than averaged away.

Precedence

  1. 1. direct bookmaker price (the book's own response) → de-vigged
  2. 2. bookmaker price served through an aggregator → de-vigged, labelled
  3. 3. our own line originated from official ratings (HLTV world ranking / Valve Regional Standings)
  4. 4. no basis → blank + flagged

Hotspawn's own crowd pick percentage is never read or published by this board.

The arithmetic

Market probability (multiplicative two-way de-vig):

p(A) = (1/odds_A) / (1/odds_A + 1/odds_B)
overround = (1/odds_A + 1/odds_B) - 1

Our own line is a logistic transform of the official rating difference, published per row by the board that computed it, with that board's rating links attached. This page does not re-fit a model of its own; it names whose line it is showing.

What each column means

Pick
The side with the higher published probability. Blank where nothing is published.
Basis
Which rung of the ladder the pick came from, plus the link.
Market
De-vigged probability implied by the bookmaker price, the book's name, the decimal odds and the quote link.
Our line
The board's own originated probability from official ratings, with the rating link.
Format
BO1/BO3/BO5, printed only when a board sourced it. "not sourced" is printed verbatim otherwise.
Flags
Machine-readable codes; the full list and definitions are on the flagged page.

Freshness and locking

This summary is regenerated by .github/workflows/exec-3b5e3fea.yml (hourly, plus on demand). The page re-reads data/picks.json in the browser and marks a row locked the moment its lock time passes, so a stale cache cannot present a locked match as a live pick. The boards themselves refresh on their own schedules — the coverage page shows each board's own capture stamp.

Known limits of this page

  • It is an aggregator: if a board upstream is wrong, this page repeats it — with the board named, so the claim can be traced and challenged.
  • Aggregator-mediated prices (rung 2) are not independently re-read from the bookmaker.
  • Rows without a sourced series format are match-level estimates; the same pick may not hold at map level.
  • Coverage numbers are of the fixtures the collector captured (0 open at 2026-10-18T21:04:36Z); a capture gap upstream becomes a visible count here, not a silent omission.
