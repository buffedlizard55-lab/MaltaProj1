What this board is, in one paragraph

Every CS2 fixture on the public Hotspawn Pick'em lobby gets a probability and a pick. Featured matches are priced from external bookmaker lines (de-vigged); matches no featured book covers are priced from official rating data — Valve's published Regional Standings and the HLTV World Ranking — and where a team is outside both lists the pick is published as qualitative with no invented number. Hotspawn's community pick percentage is never used as a probability. Full rules: method.
