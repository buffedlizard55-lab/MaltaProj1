The rule, in order

  1. T1 — external market, primary book. The project's best available primary quote (Pinnacle public guest matchups/markets). Both sides de-vigged: p(A) = (1/odds_A) / (1/odds_A + 1/odds_B). Pick = the higher side.
  2. T2 — external market, secondary book or aggregator (EGamersWorld's public odds table, other listed books). Same arithmetic, labelled as secondary.
  3. T3 — our own line from official ratings. Valve Regional Standings points and HLTV World Ranking points, each mapped by p(A) = 1 / (1 + 10^(−k · (points_A − points_B) / 400)) with k = 0.463 (the value cs2/calibrate.py fits on Valve's own published match results). The single-map figure is shown as the headline and the series figure (BO(n)) only when the payload or HLTV states the format.
  4. T4 — ranked vs unranked. When one team is in an official list and the other is in neither Valve's nor HLTV's, no symmetric rating difference exists. The pick is the ranked team, published as qualitative, with the two list checks linked. No number is invented for it.
  5. T5 — nothing usable. No pick, row flagged. (Currently zero rows.)

When a primary market exists, the market side is the published pick even if the model disagrees; the disagreement itself is a flag, and the VRS/HW numbers are printed next to it.

What is never used

  • Hotspawn's community pick percentage, its points, its prize pool and its leaderboard. Hotspawn is the fixture list and the UTC kickoff — nothing else. The collector does not read a crowd field at all, and no column of this board is derived from one.
  • Any price that is a cut-off placeholder. A 1.80 / 1.80 reading is rejected and flagged (EXT-PLACEHOLDER-180), because it is the aggregator's default, not a market.
  • Any rating a team does not have. Absent stays absent, and the row says which list was checked.

Known limits

  • The own line is a rating curve, not a calibrated betting model; it carries no information about rosters, stand-ins, map pools or practice, and it is labelled provisional everywhere.
  • Two rating scales are in use across this repository: this board applies k = 0.463 (the slope fitted on Valve's own published match results and recorded in cs2/data/calibration.json, session a174d77f) while data/8944bb84/ uses D = 372.12. The same fixture can therefore carry different model percentages on different boards. Each board prints the curve it used; this one prints the external market figure first whenever a quote exists, so the market view is unaffected.
  • T2 readings come from an aggregator page and are not independently confirmed against the book it names.
  • Low-tier fixtures are frequently listed by Hotspawn without a format. Where the format is unknown the headline figure is the single-map probability; the pick is unchanged by that, because any monotone rating curve orders BO1 and BO3 the same way — only the number moves.
  • Real-world availability of a market changes by the minute; each row prints the time its price was read, and a price is never carried forward silently.
