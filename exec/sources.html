Where our numbers come from

The featured matches on the Pick'em board are priced by big bookmakers. Most of the board is not: low-tier online cups often have no listed market at all. For those rows this project walks down a documented ladder and stops at the first rung that answers, leaving the row blank if none does.

The ladder

  1. Direct bookmaker response — Pinnacle's public guest endpoint (guest.api.arcadia.pinnacle.com) that its own CS2 page loads. Read on 2026-10-14 02:34 UTC for this session's own verification: verified reachable. Whole-match moneyline only; prices are American, converted to decimal, then de-vigged.
  2. Bookmaker price served through an aggregator (bc.game prices listed on EGamersWorld). Used, but labelled aggregator-mediated on every row, and rejected outright when the listing shows a placeholder pair — see the flagged page.
  3. Prediction market (Polymarket public API) — an outside market that does list some of these low-tier cups. It is a market, not a bookmaker, its low-tier liquidity is thin, and its CS2 events are frequently per-map markets, which are not a series probability. This board therefore only republishes what the market itself states, with the map/series distinction kept explicit.
  4. Our own line from official ratings — Valve Regional Standings (Valve's own published standings file) cross-read with the HLTV world ranking. This is the rung that covers the matches no bookmaker lists; a team with neither rating leaves the row blank and flagged.
  5. Nothing — no price, no rating: the row stays blank, the reason is printed, and it is listed on the flagged page. No default 50/50, ever.

Official sources this board links to

  • Hotspawn Pick'em board (fixture identity, kickoff and lock epochs only): play.hotspawn.com/cs2-pickems and /lobby.
  • Valve Regional Standings (official Valve file): github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings.
  • HLTV world ranking: hltv.org/ranking/teams; HLTV match pages for series format and results: hltv.org/matches.
  • Pinnacle public guest matchups: pinnacle.com/en/esports/games/cs2/matchups/.

What this board refuses to do

  • It never reads Hotspawn's crowd pick percentage; that is not a probability.
  • It never averages a market price with a model and calls the result a market price.
  • It never fills a missing price, rating or series format with an assumption.
  • It never publishes a number without the link it came from.
