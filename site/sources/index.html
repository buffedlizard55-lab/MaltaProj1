01

Hotspawn is fixture scope only

Hotspawn’s public Pick’ems pages identify which matches to list and link to the match details. Pick selections, crowd shares, displayed percentages, tiers, streaks, and any Hotspawn-derived probability signal are excluded from all probability calculations.

02

Sportsbook no-vig

For an independently verified two-way Match Winner market, American odds are converted to raw implied probabilities. Each raw value is divided by the sum of both outcomes; the overround is reported as a separate diagnostic. This is an odds-derived market price, not a statistical prediction model.

03

Exchange is separate

Polymarket prices are quoted in cents per outcome share and explicitly labeled as prediction-market references. Only an active, exact-pair, exact-date series Match Winner contract is eligible. Map Winner contracts are excluded, and exchange prices are never blended into sportsbook no-vig values.

04

Unverified means blank

We do not infer team mappings, bookmaker lines, exchange prices, dates, or time zones. If a source is missing, ambiguous, closed, or fails exact-match checks, its numeric cell remains empty and the row is flagged with a review note.

Calculation details

Raw implied probability (American odds):
+X → 100 / (X + 100) · −X → X / (X + 100)
No-vig p(A) = raw p(A) / [raw p(A) + raw p(B)]

Markets with three or more outcomes, non-Match-Winner markets, suspended or closed markets, unmatched dates, or ambiguous team mappings are not normalized as two-way sportsbook markets. The data timestamp indicates when the refresh retrieved the source page; it does not imply the bookmaker published an exchange-style tick timestamp.
