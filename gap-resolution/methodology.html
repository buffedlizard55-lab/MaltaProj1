How a pick is resolved

  1. Featured route first: a two-sided outside bookmaker/prediction-market price (Pinnacle direct → Thunderpick relayed by bo3.gg → EGamersWorld-reported bc.game → Polymarket traded prices), de-vigged: p(A)=(1/oddsA)/((1/oddsA)+(1/oddsB)). Placeholder 1.80/1.80, $0-volume and opponent-conflicting quotes are rejected.
  2. Only if that fails — HLTV: confirm the fixture, teams, event and series format on the HLTV match page linked on every row.
  3. Then esports books again by name: search the fixture on EGamersWorld/bc.game, Pinnacle, Thunderpick and Polymarket (documented per match in unresolved.csv and research.csv).
  4. Own originated line: Valve Regional Standings (2026-10-05, GitHub, line-linked) + HLTV ranking, calibrated logistic k=0.4568 with the series (BO3) transform, as implemented by the consolidated board — the % is copied, not recomputed, here.
  5. Research line (last resort, flagged): if a team is absent from the official standings, a roster-mapped estimate may be published only labelled RESEARCH / PF-02; otherwise the % would stay blank.

Hotspawn supplies fixture identity and lock/start times only — never a probability. Unconfirmable values stay blank and flagged. Rebuilt by scripts/57dbfba4_build.py every 4 hours via gap-resolution-57dbfba4.yml, fail-closed.
