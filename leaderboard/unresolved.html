Limitations

  • Crowd splits are Hotspawn's public percentages; for TWC matches they rest on 15–20 picks, so one or two players move them several points.
  • Hotspawn's streak bonus size is not published; contrarian picks can break a streak — weigh that if you hold a long one.
  • Prices move; each row shows which source and capture it used. Matches Hotspawn adds between refreshes appear on the next 4-hourly run.
  • Our own line uses Valve VRS points only (no map pool, no roster changes after the 2026-10-05 standings — e.g. PARIVISION's 17 Sep signing).
  • 61 Hotspawn listings from June–July are still marked "scheduled" but locked months ago; they are counted, not picked (see Flagged).
