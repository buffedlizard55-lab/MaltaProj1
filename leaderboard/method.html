Method

How the probabilities, the crowd leverage and the two plans are produced.

Hotspawn scoring

Points for every correct pick; higher tiers pay more (lobby cards: S x3 = 7 pts, A x2 = 5 pts, B x1.5 = 3 pts, C = 2 pts, each with a x1.25 multiplier shown); a streak bonus for consecutive correct picks; the leaderboard resets weekly (week 270: 2026-10-12 00:00Z to 2026-10-18 23:59Z); weekly prizes for places 1-10 ($400/$200/$100/$50 Steam vouchers); minimum 10 picks to qualify. (how it works, lobby)

Win probabilities

  • Decimal prices are de-vigged proportionally: p = (1/odds) / Σ(1/odds). American prices are converted first.
  • Polymarket share prices are normalized to sum to 1; a market above 98.5% for one side is treated as settled and skipped.
  • Own line: per-map p = 1 / (1 + 10^(−k·ΔVRS/400)) (Valve's VRS convention, "400 points = 90%"), then the series probability for the fixture's best-of. k is fitted daily on matched outside prices (current k = 0.31, n = 176).
  • Teams are matched across sources only by exact name or a documented alias with evidence (e.g. Hotspawn "WROTBERRY" = WBT, proven by the identical six-fixture schedule on HLTV).
  • Hotspawn's crowd split is never a probability input; the build's probability function does not receive it, and a test changes the crowd numbers to prove the probabilities do not move.

Leverage and labels

  • Expected-points cost of taking the underdog = points × (pfav − pdog).
  • Leverage = pdog ÷ (share of Hotspawn players on the underdog). Above 1 the crowd under-owns the underdog relative to its real chance.
  • Contrarian value: underdog ≥ 40% and leverage ≥ 1.5 — costs at most 20% of the match's points in expectation, but a hit gains the full points on most of the field.
  • Upset shot: underdog 25–40% and leverage ≥ 3 — for players who must pass several people above them.
  • Take the favourite: everything else. Thresholds are judgement calls shown openly so you can apply your own.
  • Protect a lead = favourites + value spots. Chase the top = also upset shots. S-tier matches (7 pts) carry 3.5× the swing of C-tier (2 pts).
