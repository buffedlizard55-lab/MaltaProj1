One day is far too small a sample to prove a rule; streak bonuses are ignored. The point is the shape: the crowd herds onto big names (96% on Aurora, 92% on fnatic) far beyond what the prices justify, so a correct contrarian pick moves you past almost the whole field at once.

Why Aurora was vulnerable

Aurora rebuilt in July: new in-game leader kyxsan (from Falcons) and Jimpphat joined the Turkish core, with ash as coach, replacing MAJ3R, soulfly and coach Fabre (HLTV, 8 Jul). HLTV counts just 62 maps for the new pieces, and kyxsan rated 0.53 in the 9z series (match page). Aurora had beaten 9z 2-0 eleven days earlier at EPL 24, and 9z arrived after four straight group-stage exits (HLTV report) — so the crowd's 96% looked safe, but the price (about 26%) already said an upset was a one-in-four event.
