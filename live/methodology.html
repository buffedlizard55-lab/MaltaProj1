What comes from where

Fixture identity, exact UTC start and lock timeplay.hotspawn.com Pick'em pages — their own server-rendered Angular payload (script#ng-state). The board's rendered text is never used as a probability.
External probabilityA two-way bookmaker market, de-vigged. A single source per fixture is labelled as such; Pinnacle (direct bookmaker) is preferred over an aggregator listing when both exist.
Our own lineElo over Valve's official Regional Standings points, scale 400. A team Valve does not list gets a blank line, never an invented rating.
Hotspawn crowd pick percentages are read from the payload for the audit page only, are dropped from the dataset by this build script, and never enter a probability or a pick.

Formulas

de-vig            p_A = (1/o_A) / ((1/o_A) + (1/o_B))        overround = (1/o_A + 1/o_B - 1) * 100
own line          p_A = 1 / (1 + 10 ** (-(vrs_A - vrs_B) / 400))
headline          outside market when one exists, else our own line, else blank
pick              the higher headline probability, labelled with its basis;
                  "No confident pick" when the two sides are within 2.0 points;
                  no pick at all when no verified line exists (FL-04)

Joining a bookmaker price to a fixture

A quote is accepted only when the two team names match the fixture pair after lower-casing, punctuation removal and an explicitly documented alias (bookmaker spellings such as WBT for WROTBERRY), and — when the source states a start time — that start is within 120 minutes of the Pick'em start. Ambiguous joins are left blank and flagged (FL-03).

What is deliberately blank

  • No readable bookmaker market → no market column, flagged FL-01 (the row may still carry our own line, FL-13).
  • Team absent from Valve's file → no own line, flagged FL-02.
  • Neither source → no probability and no pick, flagged FL-04.
  • Aggregator placeholder prices (identical both ways, e.g. 1.80/1.80) are treated as unreadable.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Prices move. Every page states the snapshot time; the workflow re-collects on a schedule and the rows are never re-dated without re-reading the sources.
  • One external source is weaker evidence than two. Where only one is readable, that is labelled on the row.
  • This is analysis, not betting advice, and it does not predict outcomes — it reports what the outside market and an official-rating model imply.
