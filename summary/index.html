Executive summary

  • Coverage: all 28 open CS2 cards on the Hotspawn lobby have a pick and both teams' win probabilities. Every card maps to a row in the dataset; the check fails the build otherwise.
  • Strongest basis: 7 matches are priced by a primary bookmaker line (Pinnacle), de-vigged to two-way probabilities.
  • Weaker basis: 17 matches use bookmaker odds as reported by EGamersWorld (bc.game). They are not checked against the bookmaker and are labelled as such.
  • Unresolved: 4 matches have no usable market line. Their picks rest on HLTV / Valve ratings alone and are listed separately with their limits, on unresolved.html.
  • Known problem: the Pinnacle source returned HTTP 500 on today's re-read, so its prices are carried from the 02:21Z capture. See flagged.html.
