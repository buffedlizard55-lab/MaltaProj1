Statistical Origination Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

To meet project requirements, we never use Hotspawn pick percentages as probability indicators. Instead, we originate independent, rigorous betting lines and match probabilities for all 26 matches using competitive Counter-Strike 2 performance metrics, Valve Regional Standings (VRS), HLTV world rankings, and series format adjustments.

1. Bookmaker De-Vigging (Fair Implied Probability)

Sportsbooks embed a profit margin (vigorish/overround). To extract the market's true implied probability, we apply proportional de-vigging to verified decimal odds \(O_1\) and \(O_2\):

P_raw1 = 1 / O_1,   P_raw2 = 1 / O_2
Total_Margin = P_raw1 + P_raw2
P_fair1 = P_raw1 / Total_Margin
P_fair2 = P_raw2 / Total_Margin

If an official sportsbook line has not yet been posted or verified for a fixture, the bookmaker line and market implied probability are left strictly blank and flagged on the Flagged for Review page.

2. Independent Line Origination Model

Our originated lines are built from a modified logistic Bradley-Terry model calibrated to modern professional CS2:

  • Composite Rating: Incorporates HLTV Ranking Points (60%) and Valve Regional Standings (40%) to measure roster quality.
  • Form Adjustment: Incorporates 30-day and 90-day win percentages across official HLTV maps.
  • Format Scaling (BO3 vs. BO1):
    • BO1 (Single Map): Subject to high map-veto variance and pistol round momentum: P_BO1 = p_base.
    • BO3 (Series): Binomial expansion P_BO3 = p^2 × (3 - 2p), reducing random upset frequency.
  • Originated Fair Odds: O_fair = 1 / P_originated.

3. Value Edge & Public Bias Detection

Comparing our originated model probability against bookmaker lines and Hotspawn pick distributions reveals actionable insights:

Model_Edge = P_originated - P_market
Public_Bias_Skew = Hotspawn_Pick% - P_market

Where Public_Bias_Skew exceeds ±20%, the community is suffering from brand-recognition bias or misinterpreting recent results, creating enormous differentiation value in Pick'em tournaments.

4. Strict Verification & Source Auditing Standards

In accordance with repo specifications:

  • Every table row links directly to verified external sources (EGamersWorld, Bwin, Polymarket, Stake, HLTV official match pages).
  • Values that cannot be verified from an official source are never inferred or hallucinated—they are left blank and published on the Flagged for Review page.
  • All team names, tournament names, prize pools, and locations (e.g. Malta BLAST Studios) are confirmed via official releases.
