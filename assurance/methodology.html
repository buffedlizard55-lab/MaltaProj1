Methodology & limits

Fixture facts, market observations and calculations — kept separate.

Dated snapshot · 2026-10-13T18:05:28ZNot a live odds feed. Source quote timestamps are unknown.

Our originated fair lines

Qualified observations use the cents displayed in the directly inspected Series Lines → Moneyline section. For prices cA and cB, compute pA = cA/(cA+cB), pB = cB/(cA+cB). Fair decimal lines are 1/pA and 1/pB. Never round intermediate values. Rounded reciprocal display multipliers are retained as evidence but do not supply the calculation.

This is our reproducible price-normalization calculation, not a quote from a sportsbook or a new independently calibrated statistical prediction. Fees, spread, cancellation/50-50 settlement rules and fill depth are not modeled. A market lean is simply the outcome with the larger normalized price.

Strict publication gates

  1. Both team names must match case-insensitively; no fuzzy matching or automatic aliases. Source outcome order is explicitly mapped.
  2. The inspected dated market must be within the fixture window and competition must agree in the retained observation.
  3. The individual series moneyline section must actually have been inspected. Map winners, handicap, totals and outrights cannot substitute.
  4. Conflicting hero/list observations and zero-volume displays are suppressed. A sum above 110 cents is suppressed as a conservative policy, not a claim that such quotes are impossible.
  5. Both prices must be finite and positive. Missing inputs produce blank probabilities, never default 50/50.

Verification is field-specific

The fixture names, displayed month/day/time, tier and tournament were observed. UTC start and timezone are unconfirmed and remain null. Stored rank inputs are not newly verified. A reachable URL is not proof of the number it allegedly supports.

Freshness and automation

This dashboard is an immutable historical evidence snapshot. Its age is shown prominently; it is never relabeled live by a successful HTTP check. The daily GitHub Actions job reruns repository arithmetic audits and primary-source reachability checks and uploads machine-readable reports automatically. It does not fetch an exhaustive fixture inventory, silently refresh odds, or auto-verify numbers. The active parallel collection sessions own those tasks.

Research still needed

A validated full-match model requires confirmed team identities, dated compatible HLTV/VRS inputs, current rosters, map/series context, multiple independently verified match moneylines, historical backtesting/calibration and quantified uncertainty. No unsupported coefficients, injuries, rankings or win percentages are invented to fill coverage gaps.

Hotspawn is used only to identify fixtures, never to estimate probability. Analysis, not betting advice.
