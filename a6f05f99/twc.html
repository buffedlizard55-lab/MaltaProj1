What day 1 and day 2 say about day 3

  • Day 1 gave the board's best result and day 2 gave its worst, and the split is not random: the two leaderboard plays that worked were the two where the crowd was on the side our own numbers said was the weaker one by a wide margin (Aurora at 73% price with 95% crowd on 9z-Aurora day 1; PARIVISION at 53.4% with 69% crowd on PARIVISION-BetBoom day 2).
  • Day 2 also shows the cost. Legacy-FURIA was priced near a coin flip (57.6% FURIA) with 73% of the crowd on FURIA; fading that crowd lost 7 points against most of the field even though the number was reasonable. A 4-point edge on a near coin flip is not enough to justify being different.
  • 9z's day-2 line is the most useful new information for day 3: they lost 1-2 to the tournament's strongest side but took a map off them and lost the other two only 9-13. The team the market priced at 24% on day 2 was not the team that lost 2-0 without a map.
  • Aurora's reply was equally clear: 2-0 over Ninjas in Pyjamas with a 1.65 rating from Wicadia. The day-1 loss to 9z looks like a bad day, not a broken team - which is why the day-3 price still makes Aurora the favourite.
  • The July rebuild is real but old news: kyxsan and Jimpphat joined on 8 and 3 July 2026, roughly 100 days before this match, and Aurora reached a StarSeries Fall final and sit 5th on Valve's standings with them. Rostermania is a tiebreak on a close match, not a reason to back a long shot.
