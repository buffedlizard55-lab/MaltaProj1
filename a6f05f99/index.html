Unresolved picks and the limitations behind them

1 open fixture(s) with no pick. A pick is left blank on purpose when no outside price exists and at least one team is missing from Valve's official standings, because then there is nothing honest to base a number on. Nothing is guessed and no placeholder is filled in.

    No source stated the series length; nothing was assumed. At least one team is missing from Valve's official standings, so no own line could be made. No outside price and no official rating for one or both teams - the pick is left blank on purpose.

Limitations that apply to the picks we did make

  • Single-source fixtures (1 of 18). These rest on one market, usually Polymarket, because no bookmaker priced them. They are the softest numbers on the board.
    • Ground Zero vs Off Chops — only Polymarket priced it (DFRAG Invitational 2026, locks Mon 26 Oct 02:54 UTC)
  • Prediction markets are not bookmakers. Polymarket prices are shown and labelled as such. Where a bookmaker price also exists, both are averaged rather than one being preferred.
  • Crowd percentages are ownership only, never a probability. How many Hotspawn players picked a team is shown alongside every match, but it never feeds the number. It only tells you how crowded a pick is, which is what the leaderboard rule is built on.
  • Valve's standings are a snapshot. The newest official file is 2026-10-05; any roster change after that date is not reflected in the own line, and such rows are flagged.
  • Hotspawn drops finished fixtures. Once a match locks and settles it leaves the public payload, so grading is done against HLTV instead.
  • Hotspawn does not list every match of an event. The Thunderpick Group B decider (BETBOOM v Legacy, 16 Oct) is on HLTV but has no Hotspawn listing, so there is no pick'em pick to make for it.
