Method

How every number on this board is produced

Reverse-engineering the Hotspawn lobby

play.hotspawn.com is an Angular app that renders on the server. Every public page (/lobby, /cs2-pickems, /thp-pickems) ships its data inside a single <script id="ng-state"> block (Angular TransferState). The collector fetches the three pages, parses that JSON, and walks it for objects that carry scheduled_start_time and participants0_name. That gives the fixture id, competition, tier, stage, points, the exact lock_time epoch, both teams, and the public crowd pick share. Nothing is scraped out of the rendered HTML, and no login or key is used.

The crowd share is stored as ownership and is never allowed to touch a probability. A unit test fails the build if it ever does.

The probability ladder

Every rung that answers is kept, and the published probability is the average of the independent answers. Each answer is shown on the pick card with its own link.

  • Pinnacle — Pinnacle moneyline, margin removed (sharp bookmaker) (0 open fixtures resolved on this rung)
  • Bookmaker (bo3.gg) — Bookmaker price relayed by bo3.gg (7 open fixtures resolved on this rung)
  • Polymarket — Polymarket prediction-market price, normalised (7 open fixtures resolved on this rung)
  • Our own line — Our own line from official Valve Regional Standings points (6 open fixtures resolved on this rung)
  • Unresolved — Unresolved - no outside price and no official rating for one or both teams

Our own line

When no market prices a fixture we originate one from Valve's official Regional Standings: P = 1/(1+10^(-k*(pointsA-pointsB)/400)) with k = 0.4598. k = 0.4598, fitted by session arena/22be6967 on 13,609 official Valve-rated matches (held-out log-loss 0.6395 vs 0.6541 for Valve's raw slope); see hotspawn/data/calibration.json and hotspawn/calibrate.py in this repository. HLTV's world ranking is carried alongside as an independent cross-check and is shown on every row that has one.

Margin removal

Bookmaker odds are converted with proportional (multiplicative) margin removal: each side's implied probability is divided by the sum of both. A pair is rejected as not a real two-way book when the overround is below 0.98 or above 1.25. Polymarket prices are normalised the same way.

Leaderboard rule (v3)

  • VALUE — The crowd majority is on the side our numbers make the underdog. Take the favourite: it is both more likely and less owned.
  • CONTRARIAN — Underdog consensus at least 38%, every estimate at least 30%, and crowd share at least 10 points below its chance. Take the underdog.
  • SITUATIONAL — Underdog consensus at least 30%, every estimate at least 25%, crowd share at most half its chance, and a strong or moderate verified factor against the favourite with none against the underdog. Take the underdog.
  • LONG SHOT — Underdog at least 20% with 10% or less of the crowd. Keep the favourite; punt on the underdog only if you are far behind.
  • CHALK — Anything else. Take the favourite.
v3 keeps v2's thresholds. Day 2 produced one contrarian win and one contrarian loss on signals that look the same on paper; at seven graded matches, retuning those thresholds would be fitting noise. What v3 changes is measurement: the underdog's chance is the consensus of up to four independent estimates instead of two, because bo3.gg and the hand-verified book reads were added to the ladder.

Automation

The scheduled workflow runs every four hours: collect, model, build, run the invariant tests, commit, and ask GitHub Pages to rebuild. A source that fails keeps its previous capture with the original read time. Nothing is hand-edited on a schedule.

Known gaps

  • Not every Hotspawn fixture has an HLTV match page; low-tier events often do not. Where HLTV is missing, the row links the team's HLTV page or, failing that, the Hotspawn fixture only.
  • Pinnacle's public feed prices a small number of CS2 leagues, so most bookmaker prices on this board come from the provider relayed by bo3.gg, and that provider is named on every row.
  • Hotspawn's own "View match" links are placeholders on some listings; the row then falls back to the lobby.

Hand-verified prices

Where no automated source priced a fixture, a bookmaker price was read by hand from the named page and tagged MANUAL_READ with the exact time. A hand read exists to stand in for an automated read of the same source: as soon as the collector reads that source itself the hand read is marked superseded and dropped from the average, so one bookmaker is never counted twice. A unit test enforces it.
