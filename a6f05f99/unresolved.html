Limitations behind the numbers we did publish

  • Single-source rows (1). Only one outside source priced these, so there is no cross-check. They are the softest numbers on the board and are tagged SINGLE_SOURCE everywhere they appear.
    • Ground Zero v Off Chops — Polymarket (DFRAG Invitational 2026, locks Mon 26 Oct 02:54 UTC)
  • Valve's standings lag roster moves. The newest official file is 2026-10-05 (eec4853d7b). A team that changed players after that date is rated on a five that no longer plays; those rows are flagged VRS_ROSTER_OUTDATED and the own line is dropped when the change is less than 60 days old.
  • Hand-verified prices. Where no automated source priced a fixture, a bookmaker price was read by hand from the named page and tagged MANUAL_READ with the exact time. It is dropped from the average once it is more than eight hours old, like any other price.
  • Prediction markets are not bookmakers. Polymarket prices are market opinions, not bookmaker odds, and are labelled as such. They are averaged with bookmaker prices rather than substituted for them.
  • Hotspawn removes settled fixtures. Once a match locks and settles it leaves the public payload, so grading uses HLTV's official results instead of Hotspawn's own page.
  • Not every match of an event is listed by Hotspawn. The fixtures below exist on HLTV but have no Hotspawn listing, so there is no pick'em pick to make for them.
