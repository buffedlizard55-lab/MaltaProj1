Automatic re-checks (run in CI on every refresh, cs2/tests.py)

  • Market probabilities are re-derived from the archived price pairs (multiplicative de-vig) and must match the published figure to 0.3 pp.
  • Every own line is recomputed from the linked Valve standings row and must match to 0.2 pp.
  • Every published rating link must point at the exact line of Valve’s file carrying that team and those points.
  • No probability column may equal the Hotspawn crowd percentage of the same side (leakage guard).
  • Every row carries a source link; every blank is flagged; every internal link resolves.
