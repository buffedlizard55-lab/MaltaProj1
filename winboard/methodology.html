How these numbers are produced

Every formula, input and limitation, with links to the sources.

1. Where the fixtures come from

The match list is read from Hotspawn's own server-rendered pick'em payload (<script id="ng-state">) on play.hotspawn.com/cs2-pickems and play.hotspawn.com/lobby. Every fixture carries an id, both team names, the competition, the tier, and scheduled_start_time / lock_time as millisecond epochs, so the UTC start and lock times on this site are the site's own values with no timezone guessing.

Hotspawn is used only to identify the fixtures. Its "N% picked" crowd figures are not read into any calculation.

2. Market probability — external bookmaker lines

Lines come from EGamersWorld, which publishes an aggregated bookmaker moneyline per match — its own match pages describe the number as the bookmaker consensus (for example, on the WBT vs ENCE page: "the majority of bookmaker companies believe in WBT victory. Odds on them are equal to 1.618"). Decimal odds a and b are converted to probabilities by removing the overround:

P(a) = (1/a) / (1/a + 1/b)      P(b) = (1/b) / (1/a + 1/b)

The overround itself (1/a + 1/b − 1) is shown on each row, so you can see the bookmaker's margin. Where a source shows its generic 1.8 / 1.8 default rather than a real price, the field is left blank and flagged — a placeholder is not a line.

3. Our own line — Valve's official standings

We also originate a line ourselves from Valve's official Counter-Strike Regional Standings (global, dated 2026-10-05). The rating difference is converted with a logistic:

P(a wins) = 1 / (1 + 10 ^ ( -K · (points_a − points_b) / 400 ))      K = 0.4568

Valve publishes its own expected-win curve as K = g(rd = 75) = 0.972821 (fit.js, priorWinExpectation), and notes in the repository README that this slope is "shallower than we'd like". The slope used here was fitted by maximum likelihood on 6,765 match results that Valve publishes in the same repository, always with the rating snapshot taken before the match, and scored out of sample on 2026-08-03 → 2026-10-05:

CurveLog lossBrier
Valve's raw slope (0.972821)0.661850.22936
Fitted slope (0.4568)0.643510.22575

Full diagnostics: model/input/vrs/calibration.json (fitted by session arena/91eff436; the numbers are re-checked against that file by scripts/verify_winboard.py).

A team that is not in Valve's file, or that matches two different rows, gets no own line. The cell stays blank and the reason is published — we never pick a row by guessing.

4. What the published percentage is

  • If an external bookmaker line exists, the published probability is that line, de-vigged.
  • Otherwise it is our own model probability.
  • If neither exists, no pick is published.

Both numbers are shown on every card. Where the market and our model disagree about which team is the favourite, the card is marked and the match is listed on the Flagged for Review page.

Confidence bands are just the distance from a coin flip: lean ≥ 54%, clear ≥ 62%, strong ≥ 75%. They describe how lopsided the line is, not a guarantee.

5. Known limitations

  • Valve's standings are a 2026-10-05 snapshot; rosters can change between the snapshot and the match.
  • The own model uses rating only — no map veto, current form, stand-in or travel input.
  • Elisa BreakOut matches are best-of-one, which is higher variance than a best-of-three; the model does not adjust for series length.
  • Only the full-match winner market is used. Map and handicap prices are ignored.

Analysis only — not betting advice. Probabilities never use Hotspawn's crowd pick percentages; Hotspawn is used only to list the fixtures. Anything that cannot be confirmed from a linked source is left blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page.
