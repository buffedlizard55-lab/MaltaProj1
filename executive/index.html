How a pick is produced

  1. Market · primary book — the outside bookmaker's own public response (Pinnacle guest endpoint), de-vigged proportionally. Highest confidence.
  2. Market · secondary listing — prices quoted through a public aggregator (EGamersWorld/bc.game) or, where no bookmaker prices the fixture at all, a prediction market's implied probability (Polymarket, normalised the same way). Labelled secondary, flagged FL-PREDICTION-MARKET-BASIS when it is a prediction market, and never presented as the book's own feed.
  3. Model · official ratings — both teams' points in Valve's official Regional Standings through the relation Valve publish in model/glicko.js (p = 1/(1+10^((Rb−Ra)/−400))). Uncalibrated, so labelled low confidence.
  4. Model · official bound (provisional) — the fallback for low-tier fixtures no book prices: when one team is not in the official standings at all, the lowest published value in that same official file is used as that team's ceiling. Always flagged.
  5. Research · official ranking line — the tier for matches nothing else can resolve (low-tier CCT / Elisa / qualifier fixtures). Both teams' positions in the official HLTV world ranking go through a logistic whose scale is fitted on this board's own priced fixtures (grid search 5-400 rank positions per 90% favourite, minimum mean absolute error), then converted to the stated series length. Flagged FL-RESEARCH-BASIS, always low confidence, and never printed over a readable price.
  6. No sourced basis — the pick stays blank and the row is flagged. Nothing is invented.

Secondary listings also include this repository's own hourly market feed (bo3.gg-relayed bookmaker prices and Polymarket match markets), consumed read-only with its own capture minute — that is where most C-tier fixtures get a price. A quote whose kickoff does not agree with the fixture, or whose price is an equal-pair placeholder, is dropped.

Hotspawn's own crowd percentages are never read, stored or used — Hotspawn supplies fixture identity, tier, points and UTC lock/start times only. Full method and limits.
