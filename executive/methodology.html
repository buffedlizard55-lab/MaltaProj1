Method

Selection rules, and what they cannot do

1. The slate

Hotspawn's /cs2-pickems, /lobby and /thp-pickems pages are read in full. The Angular render state (<script id="ng-state">) carries every fixture the board knows about, including rows it no longer paints. We keep fixture id, teams, tournament, tier, offered points, stage and the exact UTC start/lock epochs. Crowd pick percentages and pick counts are dropped at ingest and never stored. Fixtures whose lock time is in the past are labelled locked and kept only for the record. Fixtures dated months ago are labelled stale, exactly as Hotspawn still lists them.

2. Outside prices (the preferred basis)

Whole-match moneylines only, read from public pages at the printed UTC minute. Pinnacle's public guest response is the primary basis because it is the book's own price feed; aggregator listings (EGamersWorld's bc.game prices) are a secondary basis and are labelled as such wherever used. Equal-pair placeholders (1.80/1.80) are treated as “no market posted” and rejected. De-vig is proportional: p = (1/o) / Σ(1/o), and the overround is printed per row. A quote is only accepted when the team pair matches the fixture in either order and the kickoff agrees within 15 minutes.

3. Our own lines

Where no outside line exists, we originate one from the official Valve Regional Standings points of both teams: p = 1/(1+10^((Rb−Ra)/−400)), the relation Valve publish in model/glicko.js. This line is not calibrated in this project: a 90% favourite is not observed to win 90% of the time, so the rows carry a low-confidence label and the arithmetic is printed. Ranks are matched to Hotspawn's spelling by exact name, HLTV's spelling, a ≥4-of-5 roster match, or an evidence-linked rename — every match method is printed with its evidence link.

4. The fallback for low-tier fixtures no book prices

Some fixtures on Hotspawn are below the level any bookmaker prices (recent examples: CCT play-in rounds, Elisa BreakOut, Svenska CS-Ligan). For those:

  • If both teams are in the official standings, the line is originated as in §3.
  • If one team is listed and the other is absent from the entire official file, the absent team is bounded by the lowest published value in that same file — its ceiling, not its measured value. The favourite's probability from that bound is a conservative estimate and the row is labelled provisional and flagged, every time.
  • If neither team is listed, or the standings file itself could not be read, the row shows no pick and carries the flag for review. Nothing is filled in.

4b. The ranking line for matches nothing else resolves (session 1b6ae823)

Most fixtures on the lower tiers are not priced by any book, and the standings file in §3 only reaches the teams Valve ranks. The research tier therefore uses the official HLTV world ranking — the same list every outlet quotes — and only its rank numbers:

  • per-map probability p = 1/(1+10^((rank_b−rank_a)/S)) with a higher-is-better strength of {rank places − team rank}, so the scale stays in ranking places;
  • S = 211.0 places for a 90% per-map favourite, fitted on the 17 fixtures on this board that an outside market prices (lowest mean absolute error over a whole-number grid 5–400; mean absolute error 8.815 pp against those prices). Where there are too few priced fixtures to fit, the documented default 100 is used and the page says so;
  • a per-map line is then converted to the stated series length with the exact closed form BO1 = p, BO3 = 3p²−2p³, BO5 = 10p³−15p⁴+6p⁵. Series length is used only when a source states it (Hotspawn never does); where the listing that prices the fixture prints a length, the row links to that listing and carries FL-FORMAT-FROM-LISTING;
  • a team absent from the ranking is bounded one place below the last ranked team in that same official list (245) and the row is flagged FL-RANK-FLOOR-BOUND — a bound, never an invented rating;
  • this line only becomes the headline where tiers 1–3 are unavailable, and it is always badged T5 research line / FL-RESEARCH-BASIS. Where a price does exist, the line is published beside it as a cross-check, and a gap over 15 points is flagged FL-OWN-LINE-MARKET-SPLIT rather than averaged away.

Every one of these numbers is re-derived by executive/tests.py and tests/test_1b6ae823.py from the stored ranking and price files before a snapshot is published, so a page can never print a value its own inputs do not produce.

5. What this board does not claim

  • It is not betting advice, and no number here is an offer you can take.
  • Hotspawn's crowd percentages are not evidence and are never used.
  • Coverage of outside prices is limited by which books publish CS2 at the time. When the total number of priced fixtures is low, that is a coverage fact, not a modelling choice.
  • Ratings are from the newest official snapshot Valve publish (weekly). The file's own date is printed everywhere it is used.
  • Where a source refused the read, the row shows the refusal. Automation bias is visible on Flagged for review and on Verification.

Analysis only — not betting advice. Outside prices are public website reads at the times printed on each row; they move. Our own lines are uncalibrated unless a row says otherwise. Hotspawn supplies fixture identity, tier, points and UTC lock/start times only and is never used as a probability input. Values that no source confirms stay blank and are listed on Flagged for review.
