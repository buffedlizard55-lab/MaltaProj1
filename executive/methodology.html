Method

Source facts, project estimates, and unresolved inputs

1. Complete captured slate, not guaranteed current coverage

The official Hotspawn lobby and CS2 page expose Angular TransferState fixture caches. We union successful fixture endpoints by ID and discard all crowd probabilities at ingest. Both surfaces must be successfully read with no identity conflict to claim complete coverage at capture. A failed read preserves the last capture's timestamp, not a new verification time. New matches may be missing.

2. Outside whole-match prices first

Read Pinnacle's public guest feed, then secondary esportsbook listings and prediction markets. Accept only explicit match-winner markets with two finite decimal prices above 1, a source URL, open status, a capture no older than eight hours, and the exact team pair and kickoff within 15 minutes. Reject stale, Map 1, unresolved-alias, and missing-time quotes. Equal odds alone do not prove a placeholder. Prediction-market prices are normalised implied prices, not bookmaker offers or observed win rates.

For decimals oA and oB: pA = (1/oA) / (1/oA + 1/oB). Overround is 100 × (1/oA + 1/oB − 1). No averaging across sources; prefer the primary bookmaker, then a secondary book, then a prediction market.

3. Research only when the featured route cannot resolve a match

HLTV match/team/ranking pages supply identity, format and ranking evidence; esportsbook and betting-line reads are attempted automatically. A refusal means unreadable to this collector, not proof that no bookmaker prices the match.

If no accepted price exists, use both teams' sourced Valve VRS points, or both HLTV ranks. Missing rank/points stay blank: absence does not imply worst-ranked strength. Missing or conflicting format means no series-model probability. Ratings older than 14 days are withheld.

4. Our model is not an official forecast

Project VRS approximation: pMap = 1/(1+10^((Rb−Ra)/400)). Valve's glicko.js motivates a 400-point scale, but this approximation ignores deviation and assumes a per-map interpretation. It is not a Valve-endorsed series forecast.

Rank model: pMap = 1/(1+10^((rankA−rankB)/S)). S is fitted on accepted market quotes, minimising mean absolute error after each sample's confirmed series transform. Fewer than four samples uses a disclosed project default of 100. This is in-sample calibration against prices, not held-out accuracy or outcome validation.

Assuming independent identically distributed maps: BO1 = p; BO3 = 3p²−2p³; BO5 = 10p³−15p⁴+6p⁵. Map vetoes, lineups, travel, map correlation and roster changes are not modelled. 400 points or S rank places implies 90.9%, not exactly 90%. Low confidence; not betting advice.

5. Missing values and deadlines

All captured fixtures remain in the full table. Past lock times are historical, not actionable; no prediction is a verified result. Unsupported values remain blank and flagged. A near-50/50 estimate has no supported preferred side. Browser clocks update deadline and capture age; with JavaScript off, rely on absolute UTC timestamps.
