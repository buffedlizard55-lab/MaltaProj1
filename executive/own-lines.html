An originated line for every fixture

Two independent, official inputs, printed on every row — 11 of 80 fixtures carry a line from them right now. Where an outside book prices the match the market number stays the headline and this line is the cross-check; where nothing prices it (low-tier CCT, Elisa, qualifier matches) the same line becomes the basis and the row is badged T5 research line.

  • Valve Regional Standings points — the official points file, through the relation Valve publish in model/glicko.js: p = 1/(1+10^((Rb−Ra)/400)).
  • HLTV world ranking — rank numbers only, from the official ranking page, through p = 1/(1+10^((rank_a−rank_b)/214.0)) with a scale of fitted on 10 fixtures that an outside market prices (mean absolute error 8.401 pp against that price). This is what reaches the smaller rosters VRS does not list.
  • Series length — a per-map probability is converted to the stated best-of with the independent-map transform (BO3 = 3p²−2p³, BO5 = 10p³−15p⁴+6p⁵). If no source states a length, no series probability is published.
  • Absent from an official list — no floor rank is assigned. The estimate is withheld and flagged.
