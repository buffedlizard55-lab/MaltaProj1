Two independent, official inputs, printed on every row — 89 of 89 fixtures carry a line from them right now.   • Valve Regional Standings points — the official points file, through the relation Valve publish in model/glicko.js: p = 1/(1+10^((Rb−Ra)/400)).
  • HLTV world ranking — rank numbers only, from the official ranking page, through p = 1/(1+10^((rank_b−rank_a)/212.0)) with a scale of fitted on 18 fixtures that an outside market prices (mean absolute error 8.441 pp against that price). This is what reaches the smaller rosters VRS does not list.
  • Series length — a per-map probability is converted to the stated best-of with the exact closed form (BO3 = 3p²−2p³, BO5 = 10p³−15p⁴+6p⁵). If no source states a length, the per-map value is published as-is and the row keeps its format flag.
  • Absent from an official list — the team is bounded one place below the last ranked team in that same list and the row is flagged; it is never given an invented rating.
