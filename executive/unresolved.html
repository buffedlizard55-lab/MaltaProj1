Limitations that remain, in plain words

  • Coverage. Pinnacle and most sportsbooks simply do not list low-tier CCT / Elisa / qualifier cards. Where they abstain, no amount of research can produce a bookmaker's probability — only a substitute (aggregator quote, prediction market, or our own line), each labelled.
  • Aggregator quotes drift. EGamersWorld / bo3.gg pages quote a book; the quote can lag or differ from the book's own feed. Every row prints the minute it was read.
  • Prediction-market liquidity. Polymarket volumes on low-tier cards run from a few hundred dollars to zero. Thin books can print prices no one would trade at scale.
  • Own lines are uncalibrated. The originated line is a documented formula on official Valve points; it is not a market and not a forecast product. Where the fitted ranking line and a market disagree by more than 15 points, both are published (FL-MODEL-MARKET-SPLIT / FL-OWN-LINE-MARKET-SPLIT) and the disagreement is not reconciled.
  • Official files lag. Valve publish Regional Standings weekly; the newest official global file is still standings_global_2026_10_05.md. A team can be active and priced by Polymarket while missing from that file (LFO 10 is exactly this case) — the board says so instead of inventing official points. HLTV's “Valve ranking (beta)” page is HLTV's own projection including unfinished events; it is labelled evidence, never an official-file entry.
  • Series lengths are stated by outside pages. Hotspawn does not publish them; where no outside page states one the field stays blank and flagged.
  • Identities are verified, never assumed. Where a market lists a different name (Sökerorg for LFO 10, WRAITH PCIFIC for PCIFIC), the row keeps Hotspawn's name and the identity is roster-matched across at least two independent pages before any price is used (FL-LISTING-ALIAS).
