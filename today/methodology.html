Method & policy

What this board does and does not do.
  • Fixture identity and lock times: Hotspawn Play's own payload.
  • Market probability: each external source's match-winner prices are converted to implied probability and normalised to remove the bookmaker overround; EGamersWorld uses the median price across the bookmakers printed on the page, Pinnacle uses its public guest moneyline; the sources' de-vigged probabilities are then averaged.
  • Own line: standard Elo expectation 1/(1+10^(-dP/400)) on published rating points (HLTV world ranking and Valve Regional Standings, averaged where both cover both teams).
  • Combined estimate: 70% market + 30% own line when both exist; whichever single source exists otherwise, flagged.
  • Hotspawn crowd pick percentages are read by the collector for transparency, dropped before publication, and never influence a probability or a pick (enforced by the build gate).

Limits

  • Bookmaker prices move. Every price on this site carries the UTC time it was read; the Sources page lists the exact page and its SHA-256.
  • Where only one bookmaker price was readable, the row says so and is listed under Flagged for review.
  • Rating points are not Elo ratings; the logistic scale (400) is the standard Elo constant and is documented, not fitted to this slate.
  • Series format (Bo1/Bo3) is taken from the Pick'em payload when present; where it is absent the row shows "Waiting For Information" and no format adjustment is applied.
  • Academy teams are never matched to their parent organisation, and no name is ever matched fuzzily — an unmatched team produces a blank value plus a flag.

Not betting advice. Probabilities are derived from third-party bookmaker prices (de-vigged) and from published rating points, each linked per row. Hotspawn Play is used only to identify which matches are in the Pick'em and when they lock; Hotspawn's crowd percentages are not collected into this dataset and never influence a probability or a pick.

Values that could not be confirmed from an official page are shown as “—” and listed on the Flagged for review page. Generated automatically — no manual input.
