Sources in use

  • Hotspawn Play Pick'em lobby (fixture identity + exact UTC lock times)
  • Hotspawn CS2 pick'ems page (payload cross-check)
  • EGamersWorld cs2 match list (bookmaker odds)
  • EGamersWorld lol match list (bookmaker odds)
  • Pinnacle public matchups page (guest moneyline prices)
  • HLTV world ranking (own-line rating input)
  • Valve Regional Standings — official Valve data repository

Re-verify in one step

python3 refresh/collect.py && python3 refresh/build.py && python3 refresh/build_site.py && python3 refresh/verify.py

Not betting advice. Probabilities are derived from third-party bookmaker prices (de-vigged) and from published rating points, each linked per row. Hotspawn Play is used only to identify which matches are in the Pick'em and when they lock; Hotspawn's crowd percentages are not collected into this dataset and never influence a probability or a pick.

Values that could not be confirmed from an official page are shown as “—” and listed on the Flagged for review page. Generated automatically — no manual input.
