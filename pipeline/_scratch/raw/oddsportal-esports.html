FAQs about Esports Betting Odds

Is Betting on Esports Illegal?

Certain bookmakers may not offer esports as an option on their site, but it is not illegal to bet on esports. All of our recommended bookmakers that have esports odds available are providing them legally, because there is no regulation against betting on the sport.

Is Betting on Esports Rigged?

Betting on esports is no different to any other sport in that it is not pre-determined or rigged. Therefore, if you are betting on esports, there is no way of knowing who the winner will be or the outcome of specific markets until they have happened, because it is not rigged.

What is Esports Betting?

Esports betting is wagering on the outcomes of video games like League of Legends or Dota 2 which are being played by people. Similarly to betting on sports like football, tennis, basketball or others, the result and any outcomes during the game will determine whether your chosen bet wins or loses.
