Fixture identity and schedule

  • play.hotspawn.com/lobby — fixture ids, team names, competition, tier, points, and the Angular TransferState payload carrying UTC start and lock epochs. Identity and schedule only. Its “% picked” crowd poll is never a probability input.
  • play.hotspawn.com/cs2-pickems — the CS2-filtered view, read in full (all chunks) and cross-checked against the lobby payload.
  • hotspawn.com/matches — per-fixture permalink where Hotspawn publishes one. Four CCT Europe Series #12 Play-In fixtures have no permalink; those rows link the lobby and the official HLTV match page instead.

Outside market prices

  • Pinnacle — public guest moneyline feed (guest.api.arcadia.pinnacle.com/0.1/sports/12/markets/straight), the payload Pinnacle's own CS2 matchups page loads. Read by CI; tier A. Note: this endpoint returns HTTP 401 without a token from our build sandbox, so tier-A rows come from main's scheduled CI capture (cs2/data/market_rows.csv) rather than from a live read in this session.
  • EGamersWorld reporting BC.Game — egamersworld.com/counterstrike/matches and the per-match pages it links. Tier B: an aggregator's report of a book price, not a re-read at the book. 14 prices were read live during this session and are recorded with their exact URLs and read times in data/5bcfd1b5/market_reads.csv; the remainder come from main's committed read at data/8944bb84/external_lines.csv.
  • Rejected: 1.80/1.80 is EGamersWorld's un-priced placeholder. Those rows are refused and flagged.

Match pages used for verification

  • HLTV — match pages, team pages, event pages and the world ranking. Used to confirm rosters, formats, official results and name changes. HLTV's VRS: #N badges are a different, newer Valve snapshot than Valve's published file and are never mixed with it.
  • Liquipedia — used only to corroborate the 2026-06-01 Tricked→Echo transfer that proves the ex-Tricked alias.

Not used

  • Hotspawn crowd “% picked” — crowd poll.
  • HLTV “Pick a winner” vote — crowd poll.
  • Polymarket — no CS2 market for these fixtures was reachable in this session; it is not a source for any number here.
  • Any historical session price carried forward into a current pick.
