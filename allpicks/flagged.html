IR-05-11 data/8944bb84/external_lines.csv carries two different market reads per row with no marker saying which one is published. Each row holds primary_book / primary_odds_* / primary_source_url and egw_odds_* / egw_url. On 8 fixtures the two reads disagree (1079546 Pinnacle 2.700/1.410 vs EGamersWorld bc.game 2.450/1.480; 1080176 3.550/1.307 vs 3.870/1.200; 1080177 1.254/4.010 vs 1.190/3.950; plus 1079439, 1079440, 1079442, 1080178, 1080179). A consumer that takes the label from one column group and the numbers from the other silently misattributes the price — the first draft of this board did exactly that on all 8 rows, labelling EGamersWorld’s numbers “Pinnacle (Direct)”. Fixed here: label, odds and URL now always come from the same read, and the other read is printed as a labelled cross-check.
