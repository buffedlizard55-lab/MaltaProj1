Formula
p_map(A) = 1 / (1 + 10^(-k · (pts_A − pts_B) / 400)) with k = 0.463.
cs2 calibrate.py (MLE on Valve's published match results)
Series: BO1 identity; BO3 p²(3-2p); BO5 p³(10-15p+6p²). Both series transforms are strictly increasing and fix 0.5, so the favourite side never depends on the format — only the margin does. That is why an unverified format still supports a pick, and is labelled as a single-map estimate.
