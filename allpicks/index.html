What this session changed

Before this pass, the boards on main published a pick for 64 of 89 fixtures and 27 of 28 open matches. The 25 blanks were all rating lookups against Valve's 2026-10-05 standings — but 23 of those fixtures were played in June/July 2026, so the October file was the wrong official source for them.

  • Ratings are now taken from the official Valve standings in force at the match date (five periods, 5 committed).
  • Six roster-verified name aliases were added — e.g. Envy = 3DMAX, proven by a 5/5 roster match between HLTV's match page and Valve's #21 row.
  • All 94 inherited duplicate-name row pins were re-checked line by line against Valve's own file before use: 94 accepted, 0 rejected.
  • LFO 10 is confirmed absent from every Valve period and unranked on HLTV, so its match gets a directional pick with the probability deliberately blank rather than an invented number.
  • 3 expired June fixtures stay blank: Valve publishes two same-name rows whose ratings disagree on the favourite. Nothing is inferred — they are flagged.
Read this before using any number here. Market probabilities are de-vigged bookmaker prices, not guaranteed outcomes. Our own lines come from Valve's published standings points through a logistic curve fitted on Valve's own match results; they are estimates, uncalibrated per tournament, and diverge from the market on some matches. Those divergences are listed on Flagged for review, not hidden. Locked and expired fixtures cannot be picked in Hotspawn's game; their rows are archival.
