How to read this board

  • Pick = the side with the higher published probability. It is not a guarantee, and it is not advice.
  • Tier A rows use a direct bookmaker price stored in the executive capture (a fresh whole-match quote), de-vigged proportionally.
  • Tier B/C rows use a relayed bookmaker listing, a prediction market, or the project's own line from official Valve standings. Those numbers are labelled on the row.
  • Value watches compare the market number with the project's official-ratings line. A large positive gap means the market is pricing that side lower than the ratings imply - the contrarian case - and it is flagged, never presented as fact.
  • Row-level caveats (identity, quote age, model disagreement) are inside each card and on the Flagged page.

Independent research only - probabilities are estimates, not guarantees or betting advice. Hotspawn supplies fixture identity and lock times; its crowd-pick percentages are never used as probability inputs.
