Reproducible

Method

Three published tiers, one ladder, no inference. This page says exactly what each number is so a row can be challenged line by line.

Probabilities

  • Market tiers. Decimal odds are converted with proportional de-vigging: p = (1/odds) / (1/odds_a + 1/odds_b). Direct bookmaker quotes are labelled tier A; relayed or listing-site quotes, and prediction markets, are labelled B and C and never described as a bookmaker price.
  • Project line (tier D). Official Valve Regional Standings points, per-map logistic 1/(1+10^((points_b - points_a)/400)) - the convention Valve's own model file uses - then the exact best-of transform used across this repository (BO1 = p, BO3 = 3p^2 - 2p^3, BO5 negative-binomial sum). Where the official standings do not list a team, its rank on the official HLTV world ranking is used with the project's published rank scale. Missing inputs stay blank; absence is never treated as a rating bound.
  • Pick. The side with the higher published probability. A pick is not advice.
  • Value gap. project - market on the better side, published when both exist. It measures disagreement between two sources, not the truth.

What this board refuses to do

  • It never uses Hotspawn's crowd pick percentage as a probability input.
  • It never fills a probability from a news headline, a community vote, or a "feels like" view.
  • It never silently equates two differently named teams; identity evidence is printed on the row (see the WBT and ROUNDS rows on the unresolved page).
  • It does not claim calibration. Project lines are estimates with named inputs and named limits.

Freshness and cadence

  • The upstream executive capture refreshes on a four-hour GitHub Actions schedule; this board is rebuilt from that capture plus research/7759beef/sources.json on every run, and its build fails if a published market or project probability does not recompute from the stored inputs.
  • Quote ages are printed on every row. A stale quote is still published (with its capture time) only when the row would otherwise have no pick at all, and it is flagged.

Independent research only - probabilities are estimates, not guarantees or betting advice. Hotspawn supplies fixture identity and lock times; its crowd-pick percentages are never used as probability inputs.
