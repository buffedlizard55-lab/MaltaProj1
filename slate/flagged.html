Standing caveats

Printed Hotspawn times are viewer-local. The same fixture has been read as both UTC and UTC-4 on these pages in different sessions. Every time on this site comes from the payload epoch, and the countdown the page itself renders agrees with it.
EGamersWorld refuses automated reads. Its pages return HTTP 403 to plain clients and sometimes to the browser as well. When it refuses, the row keeps the price it could read from another source or stays blank; refusals are recorded, not filled in.
Rosters change between rating snapshots. Valve's standings file and HLTV's ranking are dated snapshots; a transfer after the snapshot date is not modelled and shows up as rating-versus-market divergence on the Verification page.
