Method

The rules this board follows, stated so that any number on it can be reproduced or refuted.

1. Where the fixture list comes from

The Hotspawn Play pick'em pages are Angular server-side-rendered pages that embed their whole render state in <script id="ng-state">. The fixture list, its competition, tier, stage, offered points and the exact scheduled_start_time (milliseconds since the epoch, UTC) and lock offset are read from that payload. The wall-clock text printed next to a match is not used: this project's own readings show the same fixture printed as both UTC and UTC-4 in different sessions, while the epoch and the page's own countdown always agree. Stale rows — fixtures whose scheduled start is months in the past but which the board still lists — are kept and labelled, never silently dropped.

2. Where probabilities come from

External market line. A two-way moneyline price pair from an external source (a bookmaker's public page, or an aggregated odds table) is de-vigged: p_a = (1/o_a) / (1/o_a + 1/o_b). The margin (1/o_a + 1/o_b − 1) is published per row. Hotspawn publishes no odds at all, and its crowd pick percentages are never converted into a probability — they are copied into the data under column names ending in _audit_only for transparency, and no build step reads them.

Our own line. Two independent official inputs: the HLTV world-ranking points and Valve's published Regional Standings points. Each becomes a per-map probability with the standard Elo logistic p = 1 / (1 + 10^((r_b − r_a)/400)), then a series probability with BO3: p²(3−2p) (BO1 identity, BO5 p³(10−15p+6p²)). Both official values are published per row, so the arithmetic can be redone by hand. No bookmaker price is an input to this line.

Pick. The primary market favourite is preferred without blending in the model. Only Pinnacle quotes read in this run, with exact/evidence-linked names, a known kickoff within 15 minutes and an age below six hours, can drive a market pick. Secondary EGamersWorld reports are listed for research, unverified against the named bookmaker, not used for the headline probability. If no eligible quote exists, a provisional model-only pick is possible only with a sourced BO1/BO3/BO5 format. Ties produce no pick. Missing data stays blank.

Model limitations. The 400-point logistic scale and equal-weight HLTV/VRS blend are this project's uncalibrated assumptions, not an official Valve prediction formula. Ranking points are not established per-map Elo ratings. Model percentages are research estimates, not verified probabilities; independent maps are assumed only when the series format is confirmed. Unknown format leaves series probability blank. No crowd input.

3. What is left blank

If a price cannot be read, the price column is blank and the fixture is listed on the Flagged page. If a team has no exact name match in the official rating sources, it gets no rating row and its fixtures get no originated line. Nothing is inferred, interpolated, carried forward from an earlier capture, or filled in from a similar name. Alias mappings are used only where an entry carries an evidence link, and each use is flagged.

4. Verification

Every fixture's kickoff is compared against HLTV's own listing and the result is published per row; the fixtures are also looked up in the other datasets this repository publishes, and disagreements in start time or pick are shown on the Verification page rather than corrected. Each source fetch is logged with its status and SHA-256.

5. Limits, stated plainly

Odds move; every row carries its capture time. Aggregated odds tables can show a white-label price for the operator that pays for the placement, so the bookmaker name is shown next to each price. Ratings-based probabilities are only as good as the ratings and ignore roster changes between the rating date and the match. This board is analysis, not betting advice, and it places no wagers.
