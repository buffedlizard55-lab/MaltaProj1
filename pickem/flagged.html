F-01 — The complete slate comes from the page's own payload

The lobby filter advertises 86 CS2 matches and the LoL page 294. Both pages embed all of them in <script id="ng-state">: 86 CS2 records (86 on /lobby, 85 on /cs2-pickems) and 294 on /lol-pickems. Nothing is inferred: every row carries the payload's id, competition, tier, points and start time, and we publish the raw payload-derived table.

F-02 — The card countdown contradicts the payload's own lock time

Every rendered card shows “Locks in HH:MM:SS”. Measured against the payload's lock_time on 2026-10-13 18:03 UTC: the countdown lands 75 minutes after the printed start on all 27 CS2 cards and 14 minutes after it on all 231 LoL cards (constant per title). The payload's scheduled_start_time is corroborated by HLTV's own timestamps (9z vs Aurora: payload 1791966600000 = 2026-10-14 08:30:00 UTC; HOTU vs fnatic: 2026-10-14 10:00:00 UTC, matching the printed card time), so the payload is used and the countdown discrepancy stays flagged.

Rows whose names could not be matched are left unpriced rather than guessed.

F-08 — Fixtures already started are still on the board

The payload's status field marks live/finished matches (they stay listed with their crowd counts). Their prices are historical at capture time and are marked in the Status column; picks for them are closed.

F-09 — Automated collection of prices is blocked by the sources

EGamersWorld and HLTV answer HTTP 403 to datacenter clients (GitHub Actions runners) while rendering normally in a browser session — verified again in run 38445055709 where all eight URLs answered 403. The prices above were therefore read in a browser session with an explicit capture timestamp; the fixture inventory, which is served normally, is collected automatically. No block is worked around, and no login or wagering is involved anywhere in this project.
