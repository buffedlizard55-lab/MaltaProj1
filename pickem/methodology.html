How the numbers are produced

  • Fixtures. The CI pipeline fetches the five server-rendered Pick'em pages on play.hotspawn.com, parses every match card (app-pickem-vs-card) and de-duplicates on the Hotspawn match id, keeping all source pages per fixture. That yields 258 fixtures: 27 CS2 and 231 LoL. Nothing is hand-typed into the fixture list.
  • Start times (UTC, verified). The published Start column is the time printed on the Pick'em card, read from the server-rendered HTML by our UTC CI runner. Two fixtures pin that render to UTC exactly against HLTV's own match clock: 9z vs Aurora — Hotspawn card “Oct 14, 08:30”, HLTV match timestamp 1791966600 = 2026-10-14 08:30:00Z; HOTU vs fnatic — card “Oct 14, 10:00”, HLTV countdown at 2026-10-13 18:09Z implied 10:00Z. Hotspawn's own “Locks in” countdown disagrees with the printed time by a constant +75 min on every CS2 card and +14 min on every LoL card; it is kept in the dataset as countdown_implied_start_utc and flagged (F-02) rather than used.
  • External lines. Match-winner decimal odds are read from off-site bookmaker sources (EGamersWorld's BC.Game price widgets for CS2 and its LoL listing for LoL). Every row stores the source URL, the capture timestamp and the bookmaker name.
  • Own lines (de-vigged probability). For every fixture with a real two-way line we remove the bookmaker margin: p(A) = (1/odds_A) / (1/odds_A + 1/odds_B), and the residual 1/odds_A + 1/odds_B is shown as “vig”. This is our own published line, derived only from the external odds — never from Hotspawn.
  • What is never done. No value is inferred, interpolated or copied from Hotspawn's pick percentages. Where no external line exists the probability cells are blank and the fixture appears on the Flagged page. EGamersWorld's 1.8/1.8 fallback is explicitly excluded (F-04).
  • Reproducibility. pipeline/inventory.py rebuilds the fixture list from the live pages in CI; pipeline/build_site.py rebuilds this site and lines/analysis.csv from inventory/fixtures.json + lines/external_lines.json.

Reading the tables

  • priced — real two-way bookmaker line; de-vigged probability published.
  • no real line — the source shows only its generic 1.8/1.8 default.
  • unverified / blank — no line found in the checked sources; intentionally blank.
  • The “Hotspawn crowd” column is metadata for audit only and is labelled as such everywhere it appears.
