How every number on this board is chosen

What Hotspawn is used for

fixture identity, kickoff/lock times, competition and tier only. Hotspawn's crowd pick percentage is never read as an input and is never displayed as a probability (hotspawn_crowd_percent_used = false in coverage.json).

The rungs

  1. 1 · book read directly (Pinnacle public guest response)
  2. 2 · book relayed by bo3.gg (bookmaker match URL linked)
  3. 3 · book relayed by EGamersWorld (aggregator-mediated)
  4. 4 · prediction market (Polymarket traded prices)
  5. 5 · own originated line (Valve Regional Standings + HLTV ranking, formula published)
  6. 6 · research line for a team absent from the official standings (labelled, flagged)
  7. — · blank + flag when nothing above can be sourced

rungs are never averaged; the headline is the best available rung and the others are shown beside it. A lower rung is shown as a cross-check, never blended in.

What the probability labels mean

  • Market — the de-vigged two-way price of an outside market. Rungs 1–4 above, in order: a book read directly, then a book relayed by an aggregator, then a prediction market.
  • Own line — our originated line from the official Valve Regional Standings plus the HLTV world ranking, pushed through a published logistic formula and the sourced series-format transform (BO1 p, BO3 p²(3−2p), BO5 p³(10−15p+6p²), k = 0.4568). This is a provisional, uncalibrated model, published as such, with the arithmetic on picks-full/own-lines.html.
  • Research — a labelled estimate for a team absent from the official standings (roster-mapped); published only with flag C-RESEARCH-LINE, with every research estimate for the fixture shown as a range.
  • Blank + flag — nothing above could be sourced. No value is invented, inferred or copied from another fixture.

Confidence labels

  • heavy favourite — pick probability ≥ 70%
  • strong favourite — 60–69.9% · slight favourite — 55–59.9% · narrow favourite — 50–54.9%
  • coin-flip — the two sides are within 2 points (C-THIN-MARKET)

Freshness and failure behaviour

This page is assembled from the committed captures of the source boards listed on Sources. It is rebuilt on every schedule run and on every change to those files. If the newest input is more than 6.0 hours old the build fails closed: nothing is republished and the last verified copy stays live. Data as of 2026-10-23T13:28:53Z.

What this board does not claim

  • It does not guarantee live coverage: a source can refuse automated access or a match can be listed without any market.
  • It does not treat an aggregator-reported price as verified at the book itself; the bookmaker's own match URL is linked on the row so it can be checked by hand.
  • It publishes probabilities, not certainties. Analysis only, not betting advice.
