Scope of verification

Every populated match field must be traceable to the captured official state or markup. Every odds value must have a primary-bookmaker response and match-market semantics. Each calculated probability is recomputed by tests and the validator. Any field not confirmed by the corresponding official source remains blank and is flagged.

The collector reports external access failures honestly. The build checks every internal page, asset, download, and navigation link. It does not describe unreachable external pages as successfully verified.
