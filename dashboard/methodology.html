Independent of Hotspawn’s crowd

Hotspawn provides the eligible fixture identities and schedules, never a win-probability signal. Crowd picks are not imported into the analytical schema. HLTV rankings, HLTV community indicators, secondary tipster forecasts, and tournament outrights are not substitutes for match odds.

What “our line” means

Our line is a reproducible, margin-free market estimate derived from outside bookmaker prices. It is not a statistically calibrated prediction or an independently verified chance of winning. “Calculated” means the arithmetic and its source inputs have been checked; it does not mean a team’s true win probability is known.

1. Admit only the correct market

  • Primary bookmaker evidence, official source URL, content hash, and explicit retrieval timestamp.
  • Exactly two outcomes, both valid decimal prices greater than 1; whole-match winner only.
  • Not live, suspended, a map market, a handicap, a draw market, or a tournament outright.
  • Freshness: at most 60 minutes since observation; exclude quotes once the match starts.
  • Exact game, team pair, and timezone-confirmed UTC start. Only case, whitespace, and Unicode representation are normalized. We do not guess team aliases or infer missing timezones.
  • Ambiguous distinct events for the same book and fixture are excluded. Team order may be reversed only with exact identity matching.

2. Remove each bookmaker’s margin

q(A) = 1 / decimal_odds(A)
q(B) = 1 / decimal_odds(B)
overround = q(A) + q(B) − 1
p(A) = q(A) / [q(A) + q(B)]
p(B) = q(B) / [q(A) + q(B)]

3. Combine independent books

Use the latest eligible matched quote from each bookmaker, then take the equally weighted arithmetic mean of its no-vig p(A). Set p(B) = 1 − p(A). One book gets one vote; duplicate markets do not add weight. With one book, the result is labeled a one-source calculation, not a market consensus.

our_p(A) = mean(p_book_1(A), …, p_book_n(A))
our_fair_decimal(A) = 1 / our_p(A)
our_fair_decimal(B) = 1 / [1 − our_p(A)]

We never add a fabricated bookmaker margin, pick a favorite without evidence, or assign a 50/50 prior to an unpriced fixture. Displayed percentages are rounded to one decimal place and fair decimal odds to two; calculations retain full precision in the downloadable JSON.

Blank, observed, calculated, and stale

  • Blank / unverified: no supported input. Values are intentionally omitted.
  • Observed: a source was retrieved and its response inspected; this alone does not verify an odds value.
  • Calculated: eligible external inputs are attached and the specified arithmetic is reproducible.
  • Stale: the snapshot or quote has expired. The browser hides expired probabilities/prices and keeps source links visible.

Collection and completeness

The scheduled collector runs without manual entries. It retains source timestamps and hashes, checks every normalized record against captured evidence, and publishes any source failure or inventory mismatch. A successful HTML request does not prove the lobby is complete. Filter totals, weekly totals, rendered cards, and underlying state may refer to different sets; mismatches are reported rather than reconciled by invention.
