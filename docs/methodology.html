Methodology

Data sources (priority order)

  1. Polymarket CS2 markets — real-money binary prediction markets. Most transparent and liquid esports order book; used as primary line when available.
  2. HLTV.org match pages — Betting widget shows aggregate odds from multiple traditional bookmakers; VRS (Valve Regional Standings) pages for ranking context; match metadata cross-checked.
  3. EGamersWorld (EGW) — bookmaker odds aggregator.
  4. vprognoze.ru / Marathonbet / Betcity / Melbet / Fonbet — secondary cross-check via odds comparison.
  5. FreeTips — tournament outright odds cross-check.

We do NOT use Hotspawn's public pick percentages for probabilities. They are crowd picks, not market odds, and we observed them heavily skewed by name recognition (e.g., fnatic at 90% when bookmakers price the match ~50/50). They appear only as a comparison column so you can see disagreements between crowd and market — often the source of pick'em value.

Probability derivation

For decimal odds (a, b):

  1. Raw implied: p̂_a = 1/a, p̂_b = 1/b.
  2. Remove overround by normalizing so probabilities sum to 100%: 
    P(a) = (1/a) / (1/a + 1/b)
P(b) = (1/b) / (1/a + 1/b)

Polymarket prices are already near-fair (small overround ~1–3%); we still normalize for consistency.

What we do NOT do

  • Do not invent odds. Rows without confirmed external lines have blank probability cells and are marked Unverified on the Flagged page.
  • Do not infer odds from team names, rankings, or past results. Rankings appear in notes for context only.
  • Do not silently average conflicting odds. Where sources differ materially (≥10pp), we pick the highest-liquidity source (usually Polymarket) and flag the discrepancy.

Verification

  1. Match metadata (date, teams, tournament, tier) verified against the Hotspawn lobby.
  2. Each match with odds has a clickable Source link pointing to the page odds were taken from.
  3. S-tier and Stake Pulse Beat III matches cross-referenced to HLTV event pages.
  4. Rows marked Verified have at least two sources agreeing within ~5pp.
  5. Rows marked Partial have odds but with liquidity, line-movement, or source-disagreement caveats (flagged).

Tier definitions (from Hotspawn)

  • S — 7 pts ×3 multiplier. Top-tier LAN (Thunderpick WC 2026).
  • B — 3 pts ×1.5 multiplier. Mid-tier events (Stake Pulse Beat III, CCT, ESL Challenger cups).
  • C — 2 pts, no multiplier. Small online cups (Elisa BreakOut Series, etc.).

Known limitations

  • The Hotspawn lobby is a React SPA; the full ~86 fixtures load client-side. Our scraper captures 26 server-rendered matches (Closing Soon + Upcoming). The rest are documented as a coverage gap on Flagged, not fabricated.
  • LoL tab was not server-rendered and is not in the data.
  • Odds move; this is a point-in-time snapshot (see gen-time on dashboard).
  • BO1s (Elisa BreakOut groups) are volatile and have thin markets.
