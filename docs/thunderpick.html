Held live at BLAST Studios in Malta from October 14–18, 2026, the Thunderpick World Championship is an S-Tier Counter-Strike 2 event featuring an eight-team field and a $1,000,000 USD prize pool ($500,000 for 1st place). Crucially for teams, it is a Valve Tier 1 ranked event that awards Valve Regional Standings (VRS) points before the Singapore Major invite cutoff on November 2.
