Data Integrity Notes

1. No Hotspawn data used for probabilities: All probability estimates are derived exclusively from external betting markets and bookmaker odds. Hotspawn pick percentages are shown for comparison only.

2. Implied probability formula: 1 / decimal_odds × 100. Bookmaker overround means probabilities may sum to >100%.

3. Missing values left blank: Per verification protocol, any value that cannot be confirmed from an official external source is left blank and flagged.

4. HLTV VRS forecasts are NOT used as primary probabilities: They serve as a secondary reference for flagging discrepancies with market odds.

5. No inferences made: Where no external odds exist, the probability field is left blank rather than estimated from other data.

6. All data fetched on 2026-10-13: Odds may have shifted since collection. Timestamps are recorded in the data files.
