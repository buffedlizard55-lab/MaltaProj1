How to read the probabilities

  • Hotspawn is the fixture source, not the forecast. The collector uses its CS2 match list and start/lock times. Hotspawn crowd-pick percentages are never probability inputs.
  • Outside prices come first. Primary-book odds are converted to a two-way, margin-removed market estimate. Secondary listings, relayed bookmaker prices, and prediction-market prices are identified as such and can lag or differ from a bookmaker.
  • Own lines are estimates, not book prices. Where no usable outside market exists, the project uses independently sourced team-rating evidence and clearly labels the model and its limitations. Unconfirmed teams, formats, or prices remain flagged rather than guessed.
  • Snapshots are not live odds. Every match row carries its source and read time. The refresh schedule is best-effort; use the timestamp on the row and the cadence page, not the mere presence of a link, to judge freshness.

Independent research only. Probabilities are estimates, not guarantees or betting advice.
