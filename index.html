Where the picks are

Every open Counter-Strike 2 Pick'em match listed on Hotspawn, with a pick and a win probability for both teams. Probabilities come from outside betting and prediction markets first (Pinnacle, then a named bookmaker relayed by bo3.gg, then Polymarket as a labelled prediction market); only matches no market prices get our own line, originated from the official Valve Regional Standings and published with its fitted scale and its error. Hotspawn supplies the fixture list and lock times only — its crowd pick percentages are never an input.

What is — and is not — verified?

Provisional models use a project-assumed 400-point logistic scale and independent-map series transform; they are uncalibrated, not official forecasts. Missing confirmed inputs produce no model pick. Official Valve files are read from GitHub; if that read fails, fallback estimates are withheld.
A link identifies a source; it does not prove that a quote is current or a model is accurate. Secondary reports require bookmaker confirmation. Our estimates are project calculations, not facts published by Valve or HLTV. No source can guarantee the winner.
