Unresolved picks & limitations

Matches without a sharp-bookmaker (Pinnacle) line go down the research ladder — HLTV first, then esportsbooks, then betting lines such as Polymarket, then our own line from Valve's official standings. A pick is left blank only when nothing supports one.

  • Low-tier matches are often priced by Pinnacle only about a day before they start (and pulled close to start), so they sit on Polymarket or our own line and switch on a later refresh.
  • Our own line knows rating points, not stand-ins or brand-new rosters; those are researched on HLTV and shown on each card as factors.
  • HLTV blocks most automated reads, so formats, rosters and stand-ins come from researched notes with links and read times.

How to read the probabilities

Evidence policy: every number has a source link and a read time; values no source supports stay blank and flagged. A source link is not proof of a current price — check the read time.

  • Hotspawn is the fixture source, not the forecast. Hotspawn crowd-pick percentages are never probability inputs; the leaderboard plan shows them only as "ownership" — how many players already hold each side.
  • Outside prices come first. Pinnacle odds with the margin removed; otherwise Polymarket prices (a prediction market, labelled as such).
  • Our own line fills the gaps. Valve's official points on a curve fitted to Valve's own match results; clearly labelled, with its limits.
  • Lock display caveat: countdowns are computed on your device. Use the absolute UTC lock time and row-level source-read time—not the relative countdown—to judge whether a fixture is still open.

Independent research only. Probabilities are estimates, not guarantees or betting advice.
