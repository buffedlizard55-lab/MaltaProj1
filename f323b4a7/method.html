Methodology & sources

The ladder

RungSourceHow the probability is produced
APinnacle whole-match moneyline Decimal odds converted to implied probability, then the bookmaker margin removed proportionally (overround is printed per row).
BPolymarket match-winner price The two outcome prices are normalised to sum to 1. A prediction market, labelled as such.
COur own line from Valve Regional Standings points P = 1/(1+10^(-k · dPoints/400)) with k = 0.4598, fitted by maximum likelihood on Valve's own published match results (13,609 training / 901 held-out windows; held-out log-loss 0.6395 fitted vs 0.6541 raw). Source: ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings, snapshot 2026-10-05.
DUnresolved No outside price and a team missing from Valve's standings. The pick is left blank and the row is flagged.

What is never a probability

Hotspawn's “% picked” crowd share. It is stored as ownership and used only by the leaderboard rule, which is a strategy opinion printed next to the probability, never inside it.

Leaderboard rule (contrarian but not stupid)

  • VALUE — the crowd majority sits on the underdog; play the favourite (likelier and lower-owned).
  • CONTRARIAN — underdog consensus ≥ 38% and owned ≥ 10 points below that; play the underdog.
  • SITUATIONAL — underdog consensus ≥ 30% with half-or-less ownership; play the underdog.
  • LONG SHOT — underdog ≥ 20% with ≤ 10% ownership; keep the favourite and list the underdog as an optional punt.
  • CHALK — otherwise play the favourite.

Refresh

The board is rebuilt automatically every four hours by .github/workflows/f323b4a7.yml: it re-reads Hotspawn, Pinnacle, Polymarket, Valve's standings and HLTV, re-originates every pick, rebuilds these pages, runs the invariants and a 390 px phone-layout check, and commits only when the checks pass.

Evidence policy. Probabilities come from outside markets (Pinnacle, Polymarket) or from our own line fitted on Valve's published results. Hotspawn's crowd pick share is stored as ownership only and is never used as a probability. Every row carries its source links; a value that cannot be confirmed from an official source is left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page.
