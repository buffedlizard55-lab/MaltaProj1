Method & sources

What this feed does

  1. Hotspawn fixture list. The pick'em pages /cs2-pickems, /lobby and /thp-pickems are server-rendered Angular pages; the full fixture list sits in the <script id="ng-state"> TransferState payload (scheduled_start_time, lock_time as epoch milliseconds, team names, competition, tier). A fixture is open while its lock_time is after the capture time. Only identity and timing fields are kept: crowd pick percentages are dropped at parse time and can never reach this feed.
  2. Polymarket. Public Gamma API, series 10310 ("Counter Strike", the daily CS2 match series): the event's moneyline market gives both teams' share prices, best bid/ask, spread, book depth and volume. Prices of the two complementary outcomes are normalised to 100%. A quote is not used if the market is closed, live, not accepting orders, has a spread above 0.10 (Polymarket then displays the last trade instead of the midpoint), less than $100 of depth, or degenerate prices.
  3. Bookmaker quote via bo3.gg. bo3.gg's public API (api.bo3.gg/api/v1/matches, the JSON its own pages load) lists every upcoming CS2 match with its series format (bo_type) and, where available, a bookmaker's live match-winner odds in bet_updates (provider name and the bookmaker's own match URL). Decimal odds are de-vigged multiplicatively: p = (1/odds) / Σ(1/odds). Suspended quotes, odds ≤ 1.00 and margins outside −2%…20% are not used. The price is a relay, so the bookmaker's match URL is linked on every row for manual checking (flag MF-09).
  4. Matching. A source listing is attached to a Hotspawn fixture only if at least one team name matches exactly (case, accents, punctuation, a leading "Team" or trailing "Esports/Gaming/Team/Club/Clan" ignored, or an evidence-linked alias in aliases.json) and the other team is also exact (kickoff within 60 min) or a recognisable variant of the same name (substring or initials) with the kickoff within 15 min. Same team against a different opponent at the same kickoff is published as a conflict (MF-02) and not used.
  5. Output. One row per open fixture with each source's de-vigged probability and link, the series format and the flags. "Market favourite" is the plain mean of the probabilities on the row. Nothing is filled in: no listing → blank + MF-01.

Refresh

GitHub Actions workflow feed-a0bdf924.yml runs hourly, on code changes and on demand. It collects, joins, builds, runs feeds/tests.py and commits only if every test passes. If Hotspawn cannot be read and no committed fixture list exists, the run publishes nothing (last good data stays up). A market source that fails leaves its column blank (MF-11).

Data files

  • data/fixtures.csv — one row per open Hotspawn CS2 fixture
  • data/market_feed.csv — one row per accepted source quote (prices, implied and de-vigged %, overround, depth, links)
  • data/review.csv — listings related to a fixture but not used, with the reason
  • data/flags.csv · data/summary.json · data/capture.json (HTTP status, bytes and SHA-256 of every response)
  • Archived trimmed responses: data/raw/ — the tests re-derive every published number from them.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Secondary sources cover what they list: low-tier matches can be absent from both. Those rows stay blank and are flagged.
  • Polymarket books on small matches can be thin; depth, volume and spread are published per row in market_feed.csv.
  • The bookmaker price is read through bo3.gg's relay; the bookmaker's own page is linked but may refuse automated reads (see the probes on the Flagged page).
  • Kickoff times on Hotspawn are scheduled times; real start times can slip.
